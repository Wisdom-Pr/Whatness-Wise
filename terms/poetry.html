Poetry

Literature

Poetry is a form of literature that prioritizes the aesthetic, rhythmic, and structural arrangement of language to evoke emotional or intellectual responses beyond the literal meaning of the words.

Category
Artistic medium
Identity
A mode of creative expression utilizing the structural and phonetic properties of language.
Mechanism
The arrangement of words to prioritize sound, imagery, and condensed meaning over literal or purely functional communication.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it fails to distinguish poetry from prose or song lyrics, and often relies on circular terms like 'poetic' or 'rhythmic' without defining the structural constraint.

Usually defined as: A form of literature that uses aesthetic and rhythmic qualities of language to evoke meanings.
