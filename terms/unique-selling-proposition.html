Unique selling proposition

Marketing

A unique selling proposition is a marketing strategy that identifies a distinct, exclusive benefit a product or service offers to convince customers to choose it over competitors.

Category
marketing strategy
Identity
a distinct, exclusive benefit of a product or service
Mechanism
highlighting a compelling feature competitors do not offer to motivate purchasing decisions
Resolved
Standard definitions often focus vaguely on general differentiation, failing to specify that the benefit must be both exclusive to the seller and persuasive enough to drive a customer's choice.

Usually defined as: The unique factor or benefit presented by a seller that makes its product better than or different from competitors.
