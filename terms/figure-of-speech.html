Figure of speech

English Language

A figure of speech is a rhetorical device that intentionally departs from the literal meaning of words to emphasize an idea, create vivid imagery, or persuade an audience.

Category
rhetorical device
Identity
word or phrase
Mechanism
intentionally departs from ordinary literal word meanings to emphasize an idea, evoke vivid imagery, or persuade an audience
Resolved
Standard definitions often rely on self-synonyms like 'figurative' or generic descriptions like 'non-literal' without explicitly contrasting the expression against standard literal usage.

Usually defined as: A word or phrase used in a non-literal sense for vivid or rhetorical effect.
