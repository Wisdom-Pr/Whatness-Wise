Archival research

Research Methods

Archival research is the systematic investigation of primary documents and records preserved in institutional collections that provides evidence of past events, organizational activities, or individual lives.

Category
Research methodology
Identity
Systematic investigation of primary source materials
Mechanism
Analysis of historical records, documents, and artifacts preserved in institutional collections
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish archival research from general document analysis or library research, and it fails to specify that the materials must be non-circulating, primary records.

Usually defined as: Research involving the study of documents and records in an archive.
