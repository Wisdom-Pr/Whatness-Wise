Gothic literature

Literature

Gothic literature is a genre of narrative fiction that evokes terror and the sublime by placing characters within decaying, claustrophobic environments where the supernatural or irrational disrupts the established social order.

Category
Literary genre
Identity
A mode of narrative fiction characterized by an atmosphere of dread, suspense, and the intrusion of the supernatural or irrational into the mundane world.
Mechanism
The juxtaposition of decaying architectural settings with psychological or physical entrapment to evoke terror and the sublime.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it fails to distinguish Gothic literature from general horror or thriller fiction by omitting the essential focus on architectural decay and the sublime.

Usually defined as: A genre of literature that combines fiction, horror, death, and romance.
