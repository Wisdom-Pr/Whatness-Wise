Nervous system

Health Sciences

The nervous system is the integrated biological network that coordinates an organism's behavior and physiological processes through the transmission and processing of electrochemical signals between the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral tissues.

Category
Biological organ system
Identity
A complex network of specialized cells that coordinates actions and sensory information.
Mechanism
The transmission of electrochemical signals between the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral tissues.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the system from a simple communication line by omitting the central processing and integration functions that define its role in homeostasis and behavior.

Usually defined as: The network of nerve cells and fibers that transmits nerve impulses between parts of the body.
