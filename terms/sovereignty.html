Sovereignty

Public Administration

Sovereignty is the supreme political authority of a governing body to exercise ultimate legal jurisdiction over a defined territory and population, free from external control or interference.

Category
political authority
Identity
absolute self-governing jurisdiction over a territory and population
Mechanism
exercising ultimate legal and political control internally while remaining independent from external authority
Resolved
Standard definitions often state 'supreme authority' without specifying the essential dual requirement of internal absolute legal control combined with external independence from outside power.

Usually defined as: Sovereignty is the supreme power or authority of a state to govern itself.
