Structuralism

Literary Studies

Structuralism is an analytical framework that interprets human culture and language as systems of signs whose meaning is produced by their position within a network of binary oppositions rather than their individual properties.

Category
Analytical framework
Identity
A methodology for understanding human culture and language as systems of interrelated signs.
Mechanism
Identifying the underlying, invariant structures that govern the meaning and function of individual elements within a larger system.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish structuralism from general systems theory or functionalism by omitting the specific focus on binary oppositions and linguistic foundations.

Usually defined as: A method of interpretation and analysis of aspects of human cognition, behavior, culture, and experience that focuses on relationships of contrast between elements in a conceptual system.
