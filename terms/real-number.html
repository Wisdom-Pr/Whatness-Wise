Real number

Mathematics

A real number is a value representing a quantity along a continuous, one-dimensional continuum that includes all rational and irrational numbers, satisfying the property of being complete with no gaps.

Category
Mathematical set
Identity
A value representing a quantity along a continuous, one-dimensional continuum.
Mechanism
The inclusion of all rational and irrational numbers that occupy every point on an unbroken line.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish real numbers from complex numbers, as both can be represented on lines or planes, and it relies on the visual metaphor of a 'line' rather than the algebraic property of completeness.

Usually defined as: A real number is any value that can be represented on a continuous number line.
