Drag force

Mechanical Engineering

Drag force is the component of the total fluid force acting on an object that is parallel to the direction of the object's relative flow, produced by the fluid's viscosity and pressure distribution.

Category
Mechanical force
Identity
A resistive force exerted by a fluid on an object moving through it.
Mechanism
Interaction between the object's surface and the fluid's viscosity or pressure differential.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish drag from friction or lift, as it omits the requirement that the force must act specifically in the direction of the relative flow.

Usually defined as: A force acting opposite to the relative motion of any object moving with respect to a surrounding fluid.
