Psychological resilience

Mental Health

Psychological resilience is the dynamic process of adapting successfully to significant adversity or trauma by utilizing internal and external protective factors to maintain or restore mental health.

Category
Psychological construct
Identity
The capacity of an individual to maintain or regain mental health and functional stability.
Mechanism
The dynamic process of utilizing internal and external protective factors to navigate and integrate adverse experiences.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish resilience from simple 'recovery' or 'coping,' and it often incorrectly implies resilience is a static trait rather than a fluctuating process.

Usually defined as: The ability to recover quickly from difficulties or adapt well in the face of adversity, trauma, or significant sources of stress.
