Venture capital

Entrepreneurship

Venture capital is a form of private equity financing provided by investors to early-stage companies with high growth potential, exercised through the exchange of capital for equity ownership and active strategic guidance.

Category
Financial investment
Identity
A form of private equity financing
Mechanism
The provision of capital to early-stage, high-potential companies in exchange for equity ownership and active management influence.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish venture capital from general private equity or angel investing by omitting the requirement for active management involvement and the specific focus on high-risk, high-growth startups.

Usually defined as: Venture capital is money provided by investors to startup companies and small businesses that are believed to have long-term growth potential.
