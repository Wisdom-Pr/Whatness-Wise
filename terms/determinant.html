Determinant

Mathematics

A determinant is the scalar value representing the factor by which a square matrix scales the volume of a geometric region during a linear transformation.

Category
Scalar quantity
Identity
A value derived from a square matrix that characterizes the transformation of space by that matrix.
Mechanism
The systematic summation of products of matrix elements according to specific permutations that accounts for the scaling factor of the linear transformation.
Resolved
The standard definition is often circular or vague, failing to explain that the determinant represents the factor by which the matrix scales the volume of a geometric shape.

Usually defined as: A scalar value that is a function of the entries of a square matrix.
