Suffix

English Language

A suffix is a bound morpheme attached to the end of a root or stem that alters the word's grammatical function or semantic meaning.

Category
Morpheme
Identity
A bound morpheme affixed to the terminal position of a root or stem.
Mechanism
Modifying the grammatical category or semantic nuance of the base word.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify that it is a bound morpheme, potentially confusing it with independent words placed at the end of phrases.

Usually defined as: A letter or group of letters added to the end of a word to change its meaning or function.
