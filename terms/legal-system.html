Legal system

Law

A legal system is the organized framework of procedures and institutions through which a society establishes, interprets, and enforces its binding rules of conduct.

Category
Social structure
Identity
A framework of rules and enforcement mechanisms
Mechanism
The systematic application of codified norms to resolve disputes and regulate conduct within a society
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the word 'laws' to define a system that is fundamentally the process of creating and applying those laws.

Usually defined as: A set of laws and the institutions that enforce them.
