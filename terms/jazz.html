Jazz

Music

Jazz is a musical art form that achieves its unique character through the integration of syncopated rhythms and spontaneous melodic improvisation within a structured harmonic framework.

Category
Musical art form
Identity
A genre of music defined by the integration of syncopated rhythms and spontaneous melodic creation.
Mechanism
The application of collective and individual improvisation within a structured harmonic framework.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish jazz from other improvisational genres like blues or rock by omitting the specific reliance on syncopation and swing-feel.

Usually defined as: A genre of music characterized by improvisation, swing rhythms, and complex harmonies.
