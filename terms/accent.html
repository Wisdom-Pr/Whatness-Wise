Accent

Linguistics

Accent is a distinctive pattern of pronunciation used by a group of speakers that reflects their region, social class, or native language without altering the underlying grammar or vocabulary.

Category
pattern of pronunciation
Identity
speech sound variation
Mechanism
systematic differences in pitch, stress, and sound production
Resolved
Often confused with dialect, which includes grammar and vocabulary differences rather than just pronunciation.

Usually defined as: A distinctive mode of pronunciation of a language, especially one associated with a particular nation, locality, or social class.
