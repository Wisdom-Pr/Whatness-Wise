COVID-19

Public Health
Also: coronavirus

COVID-19 is a systemic infectious disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus that manifests through acute respiratory distress and multi-organ inflammation.

Category
Infectious disease
Identity
A systemic viral syndrome
Mechanism
Infection by the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus leading to respiratory and multi-organ inflammation
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the disease with the virus itself and fails to distinguish it from other respiratory illnesses like influenza.

Usually defined as: A respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus.
