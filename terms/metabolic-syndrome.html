Metabolic syndrome

Medicine

Metabolic syndrome is a cluster of co-occurring physiological risk factors—specifically insulin resistance, hypertension, dyslipidemia, and central adiposity—that collectively increase the long-term risk of cardiovascular disease and type 2 diabetes.

Category
Medical condition
Identity
A cluster of co-occurring physiological risk factors
Mechanism
The simultaneous presence of elevated blood pressure, high blood sugar, excess body fat around the waist, and abnormal cholesterol or triglyceride levels
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify the underlying physiological driver, which is insulin resistance, and lacks the necessary diagnostic criteria to distinguish it from individual metabolic disorders.

Usually defined as: A cluster of conditions that occur together, increasing the risk of heart disease, stroke, and type 2 diabetes.
