Social inclusion

Social Welfare

Social inclusion is the systemic removal of institutional and economic barriers that enables marginalized individuals to access the resources and opportunities necessary to participate fully in the life of their community.

Category
Sociological process
Identity
The systemic integration of marginalized individuals into the functional structures of society.
Mechanism
The removal of institutional, economic, and social barriers that prevent equitable access to resources and participation.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'society' to define 'social' and fails to distinguish inclusion from mere presence or physical proximity.

Usually defined as: The process of improving the terms on which individuals and groups take part in society.
