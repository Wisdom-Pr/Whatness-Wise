Proportional representation

Political Science

Proportional representation is an electoral system in which legislative seats are allocated to political parties in multi-member districts based on the percentage of the total vote each party receives.

Category
Electoral system
Identity
A method of allocating legislative seats based on the share of the total vote a party secures.
Mechanism
The distribution of seats in multi-member districts according to the percentage of the total vote achieved by each party list or candidate group.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the system from simple vote-counting by omitting the requirement for multi-member districts, which is the structural mechanism that enables proportionality.

Usually defined as: An electoral system in which parties gain seats in proportion to the number of votes they receive.
