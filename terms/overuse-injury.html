Overuse injury

Exercise Science

Overuse injury is a localized tissue degradation produced by repetitive mechanical loading that exceeds the biological capacity for structural repair.

Category
Pathological condition
Identity
A localized tissue damage resulting from repetitive mechanical loading that exceeds the body's capacity for physiological repair.
Mechanism
Cumulative micro-trauma exceeding the threshold of tissue adaptation and recovery.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular ('injury' is in the term) and fails to distinguish between acute trauma and chronic degradation.

Usually defined as: An injury caused by doing too much of a physical activity over a period of time.
