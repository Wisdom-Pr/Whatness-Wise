Rural development

Geography

Rural development is a multi-dimensional process of improving economic and social well-being in sparsely populated regions through the integrated expansion of infrastructure, agricultural productivity, and local service access.

Category
Socioeconomic process
Identity
A multi-dimensional strategy for enhancing living standards in non-urban regions
Mechanism
integrating agricultural productivity, infrastructure investment, and social service access to raise household income and community stability
Resolved
The standard definition is often too vague, failing to distinguish 'development' from mere 'aid' or 'urbanization' by omitting the focus on non-urban spatial constraints.

Usually defined as: The process of improving the quality of life and economic well-being of people living in relatively isolated and sparsely populated areas.
