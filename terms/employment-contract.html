Employment contract

Law

An employment contract is a legally binding agreement that defines the terms of the exchange between a worker and an employer, establishing the specific duties, compensation, and conditions of service that govern their professional relationship.

Category
Legal instrument
Identity
A binding bilateral agreement defining the exchange of labor for compensation.
Mechanism
Establishing enforceable mutual obligations regarding duties, remuneration, and termination conditions.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the contract from the act of employment itself and often omits the essential element of consideration (the exchange).

Usually defined as: A legal agreement between an employer and an employee that outlines the terms and conditions of their working relationship.
