Fertilizer

Agriculture

Fertilizer is a chemical or organic substance applied to soil or plant tissues to provide the specific mineral nutrients required for plant growth that are otherwise unavailable in sufficient quantities.

Category
Chemical substance
Identity
A nutrient-rich amendment applied to soil or plant tissues.
Mechanism
Supplying essential chemical elements that are deficient in the growth medium to facilitate biological development.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it could describe water, sunlight, or soil conditioners, and fails to distinguish between the substance and the act of cultivation.

Usually defined as: A substance added to soil to help plants grow.
