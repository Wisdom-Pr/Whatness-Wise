Interdisciplinarity

Research Methods
Also: interdisciplinary

Interdisciplinarity is a research approach that synthesizes concepts, methods, or theories from two or more distinct academic fields to address complex problems that cannot be solved by any single discipline in isolation.

Category
Methodological approach
Identity
A collaborative synthesis of distinct academic fields
Mechanism
Integrating disparate analytical frameworks to address complex problems that exceed the explanatory capacity of any single field
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates interdisciplinarity with simple 'multidisciplinarity' (working side-by-side) or 'cross-disciplinarity' (viewing one field through another), failing to emphasize the requirement of genuine synthesis.

Usually defined as: The integration of information, data, techniques, tools, perspectives, concepts, or theories from two or more disciplines to advance fundamental understanding or solve problems whose solutions are beyond the scope of a single discipline.
