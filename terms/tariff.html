Tariff

Economics

Tariff is a tax imposed by a government on goods or services entering its national borders to increase their relative price compared to domestic alternatives.

Category
Economic policy instrument
Identity
A mandatory financial levy applied specifically to cross-border trade.
Mechanism
Increasing the cost of foreign products to protect domestic industries or generate government revenue.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish tariffs from general sales taxes or excise taxes, which also increase costs but are not specific to international trade boundaries.

Usually defined as: A tax imposed by a government on imported goods and services.
