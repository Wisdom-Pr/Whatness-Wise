Business cycle

Economics

Business cycle is the periodic fluctuation in a nation's aggregate economic activity that manifests as alternating phases of expansion and contraction in real gross domestic product.

Category
Economic phenomenon
Identity
Periodic fluctuation in aggregate economic activity
Mechanism
Oscillation between expansion and contraction phases measured by real GDP growth
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'economic' to define 'business' and fails to distinguish the cycle from simple seasonal variance or long-term growth trends.

Usually defined as: The natural rise and fall of economic growth over time.
