Human rights

Law / Public Administration

Human rights are the universal and inalienable protections inherent to all individuals that impose a binding obligation on states to respect and uphold the fundamental dignity of every person.

Category
Legal and moral entitlement
Identity
Inherent, universal, and inalienable protections afforded to all individuals regardless of status or jurisdiction.
Mechanism
Legal codification and international recognition that impose obligations on states to respect, protect, and fulfill individual dignity.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular ('rights are rights') and fails to distinguish human rights from civil or statutory rights, which are granted by specific governments rather than being inherent.

Usually defined as: Human rights are the basic rights and freedoms that belong to every person in the world, from birth until death.
