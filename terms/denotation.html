Denotation

Linguistics

Denotation is the explicit, primary meaning or direct reference of a word, independent of any secondary emotional or cultural associations.

Category
Semantic property
Identity
Explicit, primary meaning or direct reference of a word
Mechanism
Directly pointing to a specific object, concept, or dictionary definition without subjective context
Resolved
Defining it simply as 'literal meaning' relies on an intuitive sense of 'literal' without specifying how it functions independently of secondary associations

Usually defined as: The literal or primary meaning of a word, in contrast to the feelings or ideas that the word suggests.
