Mutual fund

Finance

A mutual fund is a collective investment vehicle that pools capital from multiple investors to purchase a diversified portfolio of securities managed by professionals, granting each investor a proportional share of the fund's total assets and performance.

Category
Financial instrument
Identity
A collective investment vehicle
Mechanism
Pooling capital from multiple investors to purchase a diversified portfolio of securities managed by professional fund managers
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish mutual funds from ETFs or hedge funds, which also pool capital, and it often omits the specific mechanism of professional management and proportional ownership.

Usually defined as: A company that pools money from many investors to invest in securities like stocks, bonds, and other assets.
