Register (linguistics)

Linguistics
Also: language register

Register is a variety of language adapted to a specific social context or level of formality through systematic choices in vocabulary, grammar, and tone.

Category
variety of language
Identity
context-dependent language style
Mechanism
adjusting choices of vocabulary, grammar, and tone to suit the formality, audience, and purpose of a situation
Resolved
Standard definitions often fail to distinguish register from dialect or jargon by omitting that register changes according to the situation rather than being fixed to a speaker's geographic region or profession.

Usually defined as: A variety of language used for a particular purpose or in a particular social setting.
