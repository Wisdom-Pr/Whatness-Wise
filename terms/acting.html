Acting

Performing Arts

Acting is the artistic practice of embodying a character or narrative through the intentional manipulation of one's own voice, movement, and emotional expression to represent a fictional reality for an audience.

Category
Artistic performance
Identity
The portrayal of a character or situation through the use of voice, gesture, and body language.
Mechanism
The adoption of a fictional persona to elicit an emotional response or narrative understanding from an audience.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it fails to distinguish acting from public speaking, storytelling, or simple impersonation, and it relies on a list of mediums (plays, movies) rather than the fundamental act.

Usually defined as: The art or occupation of performing in plays, movies, or television shows.
