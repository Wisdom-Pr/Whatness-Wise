Ratio

Mathematics

Ratio is the quantitative comparison of two values of the same kind, expressed as the quotient of one divided by the other to determine their relative proportion.

Category
Mathematical relationship
Identity
A quantitative comparison of two magnitudes
Mechanism
Division of one value by another to express their relative proportion
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify that the values must be of the same type or units to be meaningful, and often confuses the relationship with the resulting quotient.

Usually defined as: A relationship between two numbers indicating how many times the first contains the second.
