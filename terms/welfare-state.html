Welfare state

Social Welfare

A welfare state is a government system that assumes primary responsibility for the economic and social security of its citizens through the systematic redistribution of wealth to provide universal or targeted public services and safety nets.

Category
Political and economic system
Identity
A government structure that assumes primary responsibility for the social and economic welfare of its citizens.
Mechanism
The systematic redistribution of wealth through taxation and the provision of universal or means-tested public services and social safety nets.
Resolved
The standard definition is overly vague, failing to distinguish a welfare state from any government that provides basic public goods, and it neglects the mechanism of wealth redistribution.

Usually defined as: A state that is concerned with the well-being of its citizens, especially in terms of social security, health, and education.
