Market

Business

Market is a system of voluntary exchange between buyers and sellers that determines the price and quantity of goods and services through the interaction of supply and demand.

Category
Economic system
Identity
A structure for voluntary exchange
Mechanism
The interaction of supply and demand to determine price and quantity
Resolved
The standard definition is too narrow by implying a physical location, failing to account for digital or abstract markets where no physical meeting occurs.

Usually defined as: A place where buyers and sellers meet to exchange goods and services.
