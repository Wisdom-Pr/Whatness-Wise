Mental health stigma

Mental Health

Mental health stigma is a form of social devaluation and exclusion produced by the application of discrediting labels to individuals based on perceived psychological differences that deviate from established social norms.

Category
Social phenomenon
Identity
A form of social devaluation and exclusion
Mechanism
the attribution of discrediting labels to individuals based on perceived psychological differences that deviate from social norms
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish stigma from simple prejudice or discrimination by omitting the specific mechanism of 'labeling' and the 'social devaluation' that separates it from general bias.

Usually defined as: Negative attitudes or discrimination directed toward individuals with mental health conditions.
