Fatigue

Medicine

Fatigue is the progressive structural damage that occurs in a material when it is subjected to cyclic loading at magnitudes below its ultimate strength, eventually leading to crack initiation and failure.

Category
Physical phenomenon
Identity
Progressive structural damage
Mechanism
Cyclic stress accumulation leading to crack initiation and propagation
Resolved
Standard definitions often fail to distinguish fatigue from simple plastic deformation or creep, and frequently omit the requirement that the load must be below the material's ultimate tensile strength to be classified as fatigue.

Usually defined as: The weakening of a material caused by repeatedly applied loads.
