Community health worker

Health Care

Community health worker is a frontline public health professional who serves as a bridge between healthcare systems and local populations by providing culturally appropriate support, education, and service navigation.

Category
Healthcare professional
Identity
A frontline public health worker who serves as a bridge between health systems and community members.
Mechanism
Facilitating access to health services and providing culturally appropriate education and support.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'health worker' to define 'community health worker' and fails to distinguish them from other clinical staff who may also live in the community.

Usually defined as: A health worker who is a member of the community where they work and provides health services.
