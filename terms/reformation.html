Reformation

History

The Reformation is a 16th-century religious movement that transformed Western Christianity by rejecting papal authority in favor of the Bible as the sole source of divine truth.

Category
Historical movement
Identity
A systemic challenge to the doctrinal and institutional authority of the Roman Catholic Church.
Mechanism
The assertion of individual scriptural interpretation and the rejection of papal supremacy.
Resolved
The standard definition is a historical label that fails to distinguish the movement from other schisms or reform efforts; it also lacks the mechanism of theological shift.

Usually defined as: A 16th-century religious movement that led to the establishment of Protestantism.
