Income support

Social Welfare

Income support is a non-contributory financial transfer provided by a government to individuals or households that meet specific means-tested criteria to ensure a minimum level of subsistence.

Category
Social transfer payment
Identity
A non-contributory financial benefit provided by a governing body to ensure a minimum standard of living.
Mechanism
Redistribution of public funds based on a means-test or specific eligibility criteria to supplement or replace lost earnings.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish income support from social insurance (which is contributory) and does not specify the mechanism of means-testing.

Usually defined as: Financial assistance provided by the government to individuals or families who are in need.
