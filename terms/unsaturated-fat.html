Unsaturated fat

Nutrition

Unsaturated fat is a lipid molecule containing at least one carbon-carbon double bond that creates a molecular bend, preventing the substance from solidifying at room temperature.

Category
Organic compound
Identity
A lipid molecule characterized by the presence of at least one carbon-carbon double bond within its hydrocarbon chain.
Mechanism
The double bond creates a structural kink that prevents the molecules from packing tightly together at room temperature.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to explain why the presence of double bonds results in a liquid state, which is the primary distinguishing physical property.

Usually defined as: A fat or oil containing one or more double bonds between carbon atoms.
