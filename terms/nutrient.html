Nutrient

Nutrition

Nutrient is a chemical substance obtained from the environment that an organism must ingest or absorb to synthesize essential biological molecules, generate energy, or regulate metabolic functions.

Category
Biological substance
Identity
A chemical compound or element required by an organism to sustain metabolic processes.
Mechanism
Absorption and assimilation into cellular structures or energy pathways.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular ('nourishment' is a synonym for nutrient) and fails to distinguish nutrients from non-nutritive substances that also support life, such as water or oxygen, which are technically distinct in metabolic classification.

Usually defined as: A substance that provides nourishment essential for growth and the maintenance of life.
