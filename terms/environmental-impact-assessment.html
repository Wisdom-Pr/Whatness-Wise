Environmental impact assessment

Environmental Science
Also: EIA

Environmental impact assessment is a systematic planning process that identifies and predicts the potential environmental, social, and economic consequences of a proposed project before a final decision is made.

Category
Regulatory process
Identity
A systematic evaluation procedure for proposed projects
Mechanism
predicting potential environmental, social, and economic consequences before project approval
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the 'process' with the 'report' produced, failing to explicitly distinguish it from a simple audit or post-hoc monitoring.

Usually defined as: A process of evaluating the likely environmental impacts of a proposed project or development, taking into account inter-related socio-economic, cultural and human-health impacts.
