Fossil

Environmental Science

A fossil is any preserved remnant, impression, or trace of a once-living organism that has been buried and integrated into the Earth's crust through geological processes.

Category
Geological evidence
Identity
A preserved remnant, impression, or trace of a once-living organism.
Mechanism
Mineralization or lithification within sedimentary strata over geological time.
Resolved
The standard definition implies fossils must be petrified (stone), excluding unaltered remains like insects in amber or frozen mammoths, and fails to include trace fossils like footprints or burrows.

Usually defined as: The remains or impression of a prehistoric plant or animal embedded in rock and preserved in petrified form.
