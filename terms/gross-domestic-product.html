Gross domestic product

Economics

Gross domestic product is the total monetary value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders during a specific period, calculated by summing the market prices of these outputs to measure the size of an economy.

Category
Economic metric
Identity
Monetary measure of aggregate production
Mechanism
Summation of market values of all final goods and services produced within a geographic boundary over a set interval
Resolved
Fails to distinguish between final and intermediate goods, potentially leading to double-counting if not specified as 'final'.

Usually defined as: The total value of goods and services produced within a country in a specific time period.
