Freedom of expression

Media and Communication

Freedom of expression is the fundamental right of an individual to communicate ideas and opinions without government censorship, excluding specific forms of speech that directly infringe upon the established legal rights of others.

Category
Fundamental human right
Identity
A legal and social protection of individual communication.
Mechanism
The prohibition of government interference in the dissemination of ideas, information, and opinions.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the right from mere 'speech' and ignores the necessary legal limitations (such as incitement or defamation) that define its actual scope in practice.

Usually defined as: The right to express one's opinions without censorship, restraint, or legal penalty.
