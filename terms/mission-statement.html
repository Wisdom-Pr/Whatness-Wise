Mission statement

Management

Mission statement is a formal declaration of an organization's core purpose and primary operational focus that serves to guide strategic decision-making and align stakeholder activities.

Category
Organizational communication
Identity
A concise declaration of an entity's fundamental purpose and primary objectives.
Mechanism
Articulating the core reason for existence to guide strategic decision-making and align internal stakeholders.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a mission statement from a vision statement; the former defines current purpose, while the latter defines future aspiration.

Usually defined as: A formal summary of the aims and values of a company, organization, or individual.
