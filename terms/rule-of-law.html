Rule of law

Law

Rule of law is the principle of governance in which all members of a society, including the government, are equally subject to publicly disclosed, impartial legal codes that constrain the exercise of power.

Category
Legal and Political Principle
Identity
A framework of governance where legal authority is constrained by established, publicly disclosed, and consistently applied norms.
Mechanism
The subordination of both government officials and private citizens to a supreme, impartial legal code that prevents arbitrary exercise of power.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish the rule of law from 'rule by law' (where laws are used as mere tools of control) by omitting the requirement for legal supremacy over the state itself.

Usually defined as: The principle that all people and institutions are subject to and accountable to law that is fairly applied and enforced.
