Similarity

General Knowledge

Similarity is a relational property between two or more entities that exists when they share identical relative proportions in their structure or possess a significant subset of matching qualitative attributes.

Category
Relational property
Identity
A correspondence between two or more entities based on shared attributes or proportions.
Mechanism
The mapping of corresponding elements through either identical relative proportions or shared qualitative characteristics.
Resolved
The standard definition is overly vague and circular, failing to distinguish between geometric similarity (proportionality) and qualitative similarity (resemblance).

Usually defined as: The quality or state of being like something else.
