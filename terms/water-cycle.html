Water cycle

Environmental Science

The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between the Earth's surface and atmosphere that occurs through the phase transitions of evaporation, condensation, and precipitation driven by solar energy and gravity.

Category
Natural process
Identity
A closed-system circulation of matter
Mechanism
Phase transitions driven by solar radiation and gravity
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the cycle from simple fluid flow or diffusion by omitting the phase-change mechanism and the solar-driven energy exchange.

Usually defined as: The continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth.
