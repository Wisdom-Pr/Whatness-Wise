Reported speech

English Language

Reported speech is a grammatical construction that conveys the content of a previous utterance by shifting pronouns, verb tenses, and time references to align with the perspective of the current speaker.

Category
Grammatical construction
Identity
A method of conveying the content of an utterance without quoting the original speaker directly.
Mechanism
The transformation of deictic markers, verb tenses, and pronouns to align with the perspective of the current speaker rather than the original source.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to mention the necessary grammatical shifts (tense, pronoun, and deictic) that distinguish it from direct quotation.

Usually defined as: Reported speech is when you repeat what someone else has said using your own words.
