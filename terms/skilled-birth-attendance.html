Skilled birth attendance

Reproductive and Family Health

Skilled birth attendance is the presence of an accredited health professional who is equipped with the necessary medical supplies to provide life-saving obstetric care during labor and delivery.

Category
Public health intervention
Identity
A professional medical presence during the labor and delivery process.
Mechanism
Provision of evidence-based clinical care and emergency obstetric management.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between a 'skilled' professional and a 'trained' layperson, and it omits the requirement for an enabling environment (equipment and drugs) necessary for the intervention to be effective.

Usually defined as: The presence of a trained health professional during childbirth to ensure safety.
