Law

Law

Law is a system of binding rules established by a governing authority that regulates human conduct through the threat of institutionalized sanctions.

Category
System of normative rules
Identity
A set of binding regulations governing human conduct within a society.
Mechanism
Formal enactment by a sovereign authority and enforcement through institutionalized sanctions.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish law from social norms or moral codes, which also regulate behavior but lack formal state-sanctioned enforcement.

Usually defined as: A system of rules created and enforced through social or governmental institutions to regulate behavior.
