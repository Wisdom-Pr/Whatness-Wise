Debt financing

Finance

Debt financing is a method of raising capital by borrowing funds that the entity is contractually obligated to repay with interest, independent of its financial performance.

Category
Financial strategy
Identity
A method of capital acquisition involving the issuance of a liability.
Mechanism
The contractual obligation to repay principal and interest to a lender regardless of the entity's profitability.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish debt from equity financing, as both are methods of raising capital; it also fails to specify the mechanism of contractual obligation versus ownership stake.

Usually defined as: Debt financing is the act of raising money for a business by borrowing funds that must be repaid with interest.
