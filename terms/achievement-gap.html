Achievement gap

Education

Achievement gap is the persistent disparity in educational attainment and performance between demographic groups that results from unequal access to the social, economic, and institutional resources necessary for academic success.

Category
Sociological phenomenon
Identity
A persistent statistical discrepancy in educational outcomes between demographic groups.
Mechanism
Systemic inequalities in resource allocation, socio-economic support, and instructional quality.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular and fails to distinguish the 'gap' from the 'inequality' itself, often implying the gap is a cause rather than a result.

Usually defined as: The disparity in academic performance between different groups of students, often measured by test scores or graduation rates.
