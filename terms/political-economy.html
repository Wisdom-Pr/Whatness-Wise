Political economy

Political Science

Political economy is the study of how the production, distribution, and consumption of wealth are shaped by the interaction between economic systems, institutional power, and legal governance.

Category
Interdisciplinary field of study
Identity
The systematic analysis of the interaction between economic systems and political governance.
Mechanism
Examining how the production, distribution, and consumption of wealth are shaped by legal frameworks, institutional structures, and power dynamics.
Resolved
The standard definition is often too vague, failing to distinguish the field from general economics or political science by omitting the role of institutional power and legal frameworks.

Usually defined as: The study of how economic theories affect political systems and vice versa.
