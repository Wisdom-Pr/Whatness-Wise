Value (ethics)

Ethics
Also: values, value

Value is a core principle or standard of worth that directs human judgment and action by serving as the benchmark for prioritizing choices.

Category
Abstract concept
Identity
A guiding principle or standard of worth
Mechanism
Internalized cognitive framework that directs individual and collective decision-making
Resolved
The standard definition is circular ('standards of behavior' uses 'standards' to define 'value') and fails to distinguish values from mere preferences or habits.

Usually defined as: A person's principles or standards of behavior; one's judgment of what is important in life.
