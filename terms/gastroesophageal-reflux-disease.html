Gastroesophageal reflux disease

Medicine
Also: GERD, acid reflux

Gastroesophageal reflux disease is a chronic condition characterized by the persistent backflow of stomach acid into the esophagus caused by the recurrent failure of the lower esophageal sphincter to act as an effective barrier.

Category
Chronic medical condition
Identity
Pathological backflow of gastric contents into the esophagus
Mechanism
Failure of the lower esophageal sphincter to maintain a barrier against stomach acid
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between occasional reflux (a normal physiological event) and the chronic disease state, and it uses the colloquial 'tube' instead of the anatomical 'esophagus'.

Usually defined as: A digestive disorder that occurs when stomach acid frequently flows back into the tube connecting your mouth and stomach.
