Marasmus

Nutrition

Marasmus is a form of severe malnutrition characterized by the progressive wasting of muscle and fat tissue that occurs when the body consumes its own structural reserves to compensate for a chronic, total lack of dietary energy.

Category
Pathological condition
Identity
A state of severe wasting of muscle and adipose tissue due to prolonged caloric insufficiency.
Mechanism
The body catabolizes its own structural reserves to meet essential metabolic demands when dietary energy intake is chronically inadequate.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish marasmus from kwashiorkor, which is characterized by protein deficiency and edema rather than total caloric wasting.

Usually defined as: A form of severe malnutrition characterized by energy deficiency.
