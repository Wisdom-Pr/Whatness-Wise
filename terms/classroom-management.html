Classroom management

Education

Classroom management is the set of organizational and behavioral strategies used to structure the learning environment and guide student conduct to maximize academic engagement.

Category
Educational practice
Identity
A set of organizational and behavioral strategies
Mechanism
Structuring the learning environment and responding to student conduct
Resolved
The standard definition is circular (using 'classroom' to define 'classroom management') and fails to distinguish management from simple discipline or punishment.

Usually defined as: The process by which teachers and schools teach and enforce rules and procedures to maintain order in the classroom.
