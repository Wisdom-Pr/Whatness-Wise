Biopsy

Medicine

Biopsy is a diagnostic procedure involving the removal of a sample of cells or tissue from a living organism for laboratory analysis to determine the presence, cause, or extent of a disease.

Category
Medical procedure
Identity
Diagnostic sampling of biological tissue
Mechanism
Microscopic or molecular analysis of excised cellular material
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a biopsy from a surgical excision intended for treatment rather than diagnosis.

Usually defined as: The removal of tissue from a living body to examine it for disease.
