Sustainable development

Development Studies

Sustainable development is a framework for economic and social growth that maintains the availability of essential resources by ensuring that current consumption does not exceed the regenerative capacity of the environment or the long-term viability of capital stocks.

Category
Developmental framework
Identity
A strategy for global growth and resource management
Mechanism
Balancing current economic and social requirements with the long-term preservation of ecological and capital resources
Resolved
The standard definition is a normative goal rather than a functional mechanism, failing to distinguish the concept from general 'responsible management' or 'conservation'.

Usually defined as: Development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
