Environment

Environmental Science

Environment is the aggregate of all external physical, chemical, and biotic factors that surround and influence the survival, development, and functional state of an organism or system.

Category
Systemic context
Identity
The aggregate of physical, chemical, and biotic factors surrounding an organism or ecological community.
Mechanism
Interaction between external influences and the internal biological or social processes of an entity.
Resolved
The standard definition is overly broad and fails to distinguish between the 'environment' of a single organism and the 'environment' of a complex social or industrial system.

Usually defined as: The surroundings or conditions in which a person, animal, or plant lives or operates.
