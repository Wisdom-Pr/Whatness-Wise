Civil liberties

Political Science

Civil liberties are the specific individual freedoms protected by constitutional or statutory limitations that restrict the government from interfering with personal conduct.

Category
Legal and political protection
Identity
Constraints on governmental authority regarding individual conduct.
Mechanism
Constitutional or statutory limitations on state power.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish civil liberties from civil rights, which are positive protections against discrimination rather than negative restraints on government power.

Usually defined as: Individual rights protected by law from government interference.
