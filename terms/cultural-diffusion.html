Cultural diffusion

Anthropology

Cultural diffusion is the process by which cultural elements, such as ideas, styles, or technologies, spread from one society or social group to another through direct contact, migration, or media.

Category
Social process
Identity
The transmission of cultural elements between distinct populations.
Mechanism
Social interaction, migration, trade, or communication technology.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish diffusion from simple social change or cultural evolution, and it ignores the mechanism of movement across boundaries.

Usually defined as: The spread of cultural beliefs and social activities from one group to another.
