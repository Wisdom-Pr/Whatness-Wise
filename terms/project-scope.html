Project scope

Project Management

Project scope is the defined boundary of work that specifies exactly which tasks and deliverables are included in a project to ensure that only authorized efforts are performed.

Category
Project management boundary
Identity
The defined limit of work required to complete a project.
Mechanism
Explicitly documenting included and excluded tasks to prevent unauthorized expansion.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the 'scope' (the boundary) with the 'project plan' (the document) and fails to explicitly distinguish it from 'scope creep' or 'work breakdown'.

Usually defined as: The part of project planning that involves determining and documenting a list of specific project goals, deliverables, tasks, costs, and deadlines.
