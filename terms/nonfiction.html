Nonfiction

Literature
Also: non-fiction

Nonfiction is a category of literature composed of prose narratives that claim to represent verifiable historical, biographical, or scientific reality rather than imagined events.

Category
Literary genre
Identity
Prose narrative
Mechanism
Correspondence to verifiable external reality
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish nonfiction from journalism or academic reportage, which are also based on facts; it also risks circularity by implying that 'real' is the only marker of the genre.

Usually defined as: Writing that is based on facts, real events, and real people, such as biography or history.
