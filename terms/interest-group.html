Interest group

Political Science

An interest group is a collective organization that seeks to influence public policy through targeted advocacy and lobbying without the primary goal of winning elective office.

Category
Political organization
Identity
A collective entity formed by individuals with shared policy preferences
Mechanism
Advocacy and lobbying directed at government officials to influence legislative or regulatory outcomes
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish interest groups from political parties, which also share interests and influence policy but do so by seeking to win elections and control government offices.

Usually defined as: An organization of people who share a common interest and work together to influence government policy.
