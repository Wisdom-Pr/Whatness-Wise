Terrorism

Political Science

Terrorism is a strategy of asymmetric violence that utilizes the deliberate targeting of non-combatants to create widespread fear as a mechanism for coercing political or ideological concessions from a governing authority or society.

Category
Political violence
Identity
A tactic of asymmetric warfare
Mechanism
The systematic use of violence or the threat thereof to induce fear in a target population to coerce a government or society into political change.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish terrorism from war crimes or general criminal violence, as it lacks a clear focus on the psychological objective of coercion via fear.

Usually defined as: The unlawful use of violence and intimidation, especially against civilians, in the pursuit of political aims.
