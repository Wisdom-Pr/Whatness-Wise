Mindfulness

Mental Health

Mindfulness is a state of mental awareness produced by the intentional and non-judgmental observation of one's present thoughts, sensations, and environment.

Category
Mental state
Identity
A form of sustained, non-judgmental attention to the present moment.
Mechanism
The intentional regulation of focus to observe internal and external experiences without reactive cognitive processing.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it fails to distinguish mindfulness from simple awareness, observation, or general consciousness, which do not require the specific non-judgmental or regulatory components.

Usually defined as: The quality or state of being conscious or aware of something.
