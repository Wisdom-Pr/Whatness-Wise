Transformer

Electrical Engineering

A transformer is an electrical device that changes the voltage level of an alternating current by transferring energy between circuits through electromagnetic induction in shared magnetic coils.

Category
Electrical component
Identity
A passive device that changes the voltage level of an alternating current.
Mechanism
Electromagnetic induction between two or more coupled coils sharing a common magnetic flux.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish transformers from other inductive devices like motors or generators, which also use induction but convert energy between electrical and mechanical forms rather than strictly between electrical circuits.

Usually defined as: A device that transfers electrical energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction.
