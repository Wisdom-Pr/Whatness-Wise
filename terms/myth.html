Myth

Literature

Myth is a symbolic narrative that establishes the foundational values and cosmic order of a culture by projecting its beliefs onto archetypal characters and events.

Category
Narrative construct
Identity
A symbolic narrative that functions as a foundational cultural truth for a specific community.
Mechanism
The articulation of a society's core values, origins, or natural phenomena through non-historical, often supernatural, archetypal characters and events.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates myth with 'falsehood' or 'fable,' failing to distinguish it from folklore or legend by its specific role in validating social order and cosmic meaning.

Usually defined as: A traditional story, especially one concerning the early history of a people or explaining some natural or social phenomenon, and typically involving supernatural beings or events.
