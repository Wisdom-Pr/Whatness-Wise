Foreshadowing

Literature

Foreshadowing is a narrative technique that provides subtle, advance cues about future plot developments, ensuring that the eventual outcome is grounded in earlier, carefully placed information.

Category
Literary device
Identity
A narrative technique used to provide advance cues about future plot developments.
Mechanism
The strategic placement of symbolic, dialogue-based, or situational clues that establish narrative anticipation without revealing the outcome.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish foreshadowing from 'red herrings' (false clues) or 'flash-forwards' (explicit glimpses), and it often ignores the structural necessity of these cues being validated by later events.

Usually defined as: A literary device where an author hints at what is to come later in the story.
