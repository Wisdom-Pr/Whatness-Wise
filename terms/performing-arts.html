Performing Arts

Performing arts is a category of creative expression that conveys narrative or aesthetic meaning through the live, intentional action of a performer's body or voice before an audience.

Category
Artistic discipline
Identity
A mode of creative expression realized through the physical presence and actions of a performer.
Mechanism
The immediate, live interaction between the performer's body or voice and an audience within a shared space and time.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish performing arts from public speaking or sports, which also involve live performance but lack the primary intent of aesthetic or narrative representation.

Usually defined as: Art forms that involve performance by actors or musicians in front of a live audience.
