Language

Linguistics

Language is a structured system of arbitrary symbols and grammatical rules that enables individuals to construct and communicate an infinite variety of meanings.

Category
structured system of communication
Identity
system of arbitrary symbols governed by grammatical rules
Mechanism
combining discrete symbols according to syntactic rules to generate open-ended expressions
Resolved
Standard definitions focus broadly on general communication, failing to distinguish human language from simple signal systems or animal communication that lack recursive grammar and infinite expressiveness.

Usually defined as: A system of communication used by a particular country or community.
