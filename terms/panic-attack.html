Panic attack

Mental Health

A panic attack is an acute episode of intense physiological and cognitive distress produced by the sudden, inappropriate activation of the body's fight-or-flight stress response in the absence of an immediate external threat.

Category
Psychological event
Identity
An acute episode of intense physiological and cognitive distress
Mechanism
The activation of the body's 'fight-or-flight' stress response system in the absence of an immediate external threat
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the phenomenon from generalized anxiety or phobias by omitting the specific requirement of a sudden, discrete onset of peak intensity.

Usually defined as: A sudden episode of intense fear that triggers severe physical reactions when there is no real danger or apparent cause.
