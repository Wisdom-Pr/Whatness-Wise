Physical therapy

Exercise Science
Also: physiotherapy

Physical therapy is a healthcare profession that restores and maintains human mobility and function through the clinical application of therapeutic exercise, manual techniques, and physical modalities.

Category
Clinical practice
Identity
A healthcare profession focused on the restoration and maintenance of physical function and mobility.
Mechanism
The application of mechanical force, therapeutic exercise, and physical modalities to address musculoskeletal and neurological impairments.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it conflates the profession with the modalities themselves and fails to distinguish it from other manual therapies like massage therapy or chiropractic care.

Usually defined as: The treatment of disease, injury, or deformity by physical methods such as massage, heat treatment, and exercise.
