Operations research

Operations Research

Operations research is the application of mathematical modeling, statistical analysis, and optimization algorithms to identify the most efficient decision-making strategy for complex organizational systems.

Category
Analytical discipline
Identity
The application of advanced analytical methods to optimize decision-making within complex organizational systems.
Mechanism
Mathematical modeling, statistical analysis, and algorithmic optimization to identify the most efficient course of action among competing alternatives.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it could describe any scientific management or engineering field without distinguishing the reliance on mathematical optimization and quantitative modeling.

Usually defined as: Operations research is the application of scientific methods to the study of complex systems to improve decision-making.
