Memory care

Reproductive and Family Health

Memory care is a specialized residential service that provides structured, secure supervision and cognitive-focused programming specifically designed to manage the unique safety and behavioral needs of individuals with progressive dementia.

Category
Specialized long-term care
Identity
A residential or clinical service environment
Mechanism
Providing structured supervision and cognitive support tailored to the specific needs of individuals with progressive memory-impairing conditions
Resolved
The standard definition is circular and fails to distinguish the service from general assisted living, which also provides care for people who happen to have memory loss.

Usually defined as: A form of long-term care designed for people with Alzheimer's or other forms of dementia.
