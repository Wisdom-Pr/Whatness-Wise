Creep (materials)

Construction and Materials
Also: creep

Creep is the time-dependent, permanent deformation of a solid material under a constant load, occurring primarily at elevated temperatures through atomic diffusion and grain boundary movement.

Category
Physical phenomenon
Identity
Time-dependent, permanent deformation of a solid material under constant mechanical stress.
Mechanism
Diffusion of atoms, dislocation climb, and grain boundary sliding occurring at elevated temperatures relative to the material's melting point.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify the requirement of elevated temperatures, which distinguishes creep from simple plastic deformation or fatigue.

Usually defined as: The tendency of a solid material to move slowly or deform permanently under the influence of mechanical stresses.
