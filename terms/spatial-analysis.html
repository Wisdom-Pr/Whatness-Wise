Spatial analysis

Geography

Spatial analysis is a set of computational and statistical methods that identify patterns, trends, or relationships in data by evaluating the geographic location and geometric properties of features.

Category
Analytical methodology
Identity
A set of computational and statistical methods for processing location-based data.
Mechanism
Applying geometric, topological, or statistical algorithms to identify patterns, trends, or relationships that are inherent to the geographic distribution of objects.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the term 'spatial' to define 'spatial analysis' and fails to distinguish the process from simple data visualization or mapping.

Usually defined as: The process of examining the locations, attributes, and relationships of features in spatial data through techniques which implement a wide variety of analytical operations.
