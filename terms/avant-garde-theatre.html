Avant-garde theatre

Performing Arts
Also: avant-garde theater

Avant-garde theatre is a mode of performance that rejects conventional narrative and representational staging to challenge established artistic and social norms through the systematic subversion of traditional theatrical expectations.

Category
Artistic movement
Identity
A mode of theatrical performance that intentionally rejects conventional narrative structures and representational staging.
Mechanism
The systematic subversion of established artistic conventions to provoke audience engagement and challenge socio-political status quos.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'theatre' to define 'theatre' and relies on vague terms like 'pushes boundaries' which could describe any new play.

Usually defined as: Experimental or innovative theatre that pushes the boundaries of traditional performance and social norms.
