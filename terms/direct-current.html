Direct current

Electrical Engineering
Also: DC

Direct current is the unidirectional flow of electric charge carriers produced by a constant potential difference maintained between two points in a conductive path.

Category
Electric charge flow
Identity
A unidirectional movement of electric charge carriers
Mechanism
A constant potential difference maintained between two points in a conductive path
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between steady-state direct current and pulsating unidirectional current, and it lacks the requirement of a constant potential difference.

Usually defined as: An electric current that flows in one direction only.
