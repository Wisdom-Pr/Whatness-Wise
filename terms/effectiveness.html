Effectiveness

Business

Effectiveness is the degree to which an action or process achieves its intended outcome, independent of the resources consumed to produce it.

Category
Performance metric
Identity
The extent to which an output aligns with a predefined objective.
Mechanism
Comparing realized outcomes against established goals.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'successful' and fails to distinguish effectiveness from efficiency (the ratio of output to input).

Usually defined as: The degree to which something is successful in producing a desired result.
