Hypothesis

Science

Hypothesis is a provisional, testable statement regarding the relationship between variables that must be capable of being proven false through empirical investigation.

Category
Provisional proposition
Identity
A testable statement regarding the relationship between variables.
Mechanism
Empirical observation and deductive reasoning.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a hypothesis from a mere guess or a theory, and it often omits the requirement of falsifiability.

Usually defined as: A proposed explanation for a phenomenon, often used as a starting point for further investigation.
