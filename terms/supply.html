Supply

Economics

Supply is the total schedule of quantities of a good or service that producers are willing and able to offer for sale at every possible price point within a given period.

Category
Economic quantity
Identity
The total quantity of a specific commodity available for exchange in a market.
Mechanism
The relationship between the price of a good and the quantity producers are willing to provide, governed by the law of supply.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates 'supply' (the entire schedule/curve) with 'quantity supplied' (a single point on that curve).

Usually defined as: The amount of a good or service that producers are willing and able to offer for sale at a given price.
