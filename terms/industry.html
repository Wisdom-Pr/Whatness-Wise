Industry

Business

Industry is the systematic, large-scale production of goods or provision of services that transforms raw materials or labor into marketable output through specialized processes.

Category
Economic activity
Identity
The systematic, large-scale production of goods or provision of services.
Mechanism
The organized transformation of raw materials or labor into marketable output through specialized processes.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish industry from general 'work' or 'business' by omitting the requirement for systematic, large-scale organization and specialized production.

Usually defined as: The production of goods or related services within an economy.
