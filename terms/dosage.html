Dosage

Pharmacology

Dosage is the prescribed quantity of a substance administered to a biological system, calculated as a ratio of the amount of the agent to the body mass or physiological capacity of the recipient.

Category
Quantity
Identity
A specific measure of a therapeutic or toxic substance administered to a biological system.
Mechanism
The calculation of substance quantity relative to the body mass or physiological capacity of the recipient to achieve a desired effect.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish dosage from 'dose' (a single administration) and ignores the critical relationship between the substance amount and the biological recipient's size or tolerance.

Usually defined as: The amount of a medicine or drug taken at one time.
