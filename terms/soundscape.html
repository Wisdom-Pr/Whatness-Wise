Soundscape

Music

Soundscape is the total acoustic environment of a location, consisting of natural, human, and technological sounds that together characterize the unique identity of that space.

Category
Acoustic environment
Identity
The totality of auditory elements within a physical space.
Mechanism
The interaction between physical sound sources and the listener's perception of the environment.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between a mere collection of sounds and the structured, contextual, and temporal character that defines a soundscape as a distinct field of study.

Usually defined as: The collection of sounds that can be heard in a particular location.
