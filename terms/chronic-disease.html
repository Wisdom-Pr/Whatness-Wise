Chronic disease

Medicine

Chronic disease is a long-term physiological impairment lasting three months or more that requires ongoing medical management to control its progression rather than resolving through a single acute treatment.

Category
Medical condition
Identity
A long-term health impairment or deviation from normal physiological function.
Mechanism
Persistent progression over an extended duration, typically defined as three months or longer, often requiring ongoing medical management rather than resolving through a single acute intervention.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'disease' to define 'chronic disease' and fails to specify the temporal threshold (three months) that distinguishes chronic conditions from acute or self-limiting illnesses.

Usually defined as: A disease that persists for a long time or constantly recurs.
