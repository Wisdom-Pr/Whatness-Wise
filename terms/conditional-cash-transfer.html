Conditional cash transfer

Social Welfare

Conditional cash transfer is a social welfare program that provides direct financial assistance to households contingent upon the recipients meeting specific requirements related to health, education, or nutrition.

Category
Social welfare policy
Identity
A mechanism for poverty reduction
Mechanism
The provision of direct financial assistance contingent upon the recipient's fulfillment of specific behavioral requirements related to human capital development.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the mechanism (conditional requirements) from the outcome (poverty reduction) and omits the specific goal of human capital investment.

Usually defined as: A social welfare program that gives money to poor families on the condition that they meet certain requirements.
