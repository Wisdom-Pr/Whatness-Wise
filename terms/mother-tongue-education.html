Mother-tongue education

Education
Also: mother tongue instruction

Mother-tongue education is an instructional approach that uses the learner's primary language as the medium of teaching to ensure that new concepts are built upon the foundation of the student's existing linguistic and cognitive framework.

Category
Educational methodology
Identity
Instructional delivery system
Mechanism
Utilizing the learner's primary language as the medium of instruction to facilitate cognitive and linguistic development.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the concept from bilingual education or simple translation, and it misses the pedagogical mechanism of leveraging existing conceptual frameworks.

Usually defined as: Education provided in the student's first language.
