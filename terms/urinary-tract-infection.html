Urinary tract infection

Medicine
Also: UTI

A urinary tract infection is a pathological condition caused by the colonization and proliferation of microorganisms within the urinary system that triggers an inflammatory response in the host tissue.

Category
Pathological condition
Identity
A microbial colonization of the urinary tract
Mechanism
Pathogens overcoming host defenses to proliferate within the urinary system
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the term 'infection' to define the condition, and it fails to distinguish between colonization and symptomatic disease.

Usually defined as: An infection in any part of the urinary system, including kidneys, ureters, bladder, and urethra.
