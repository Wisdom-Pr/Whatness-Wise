Well-being

Mental Health
Also: wellbeing, emotional wellbeing

Well-being is a multidimensional state of existence that integrates physical health, psychological stability, and social functionality to provide a sustained sense of life satisfaction.

Category
State of existence
Identity
A multidimensional condition of holistic health and life satisfaction
Mechanism
the integration of physical health, psychological stability, and social functionality
Resolved
The standard definition is circular ('well-being' contains 'well') and fails to discriminate between fleeting moods and sustained life quality.

Usually defined as: The state of being comfortable, healthy, or happy.
