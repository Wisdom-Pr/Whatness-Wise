Theatre

Performing Arts
Also: theater

Theatre is a collaborative performing art that uses live actors to enact narrative or conceptual events in real-time before an audience.

Category
Fine Art
Identity
A collaborative performance medium
Mechanism
The enactment of narrative or non-narrative events by live performers before an audience
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the art form with the physical building or the script, failing to distinguish it from film or literature.

Usually defined as: A collaborative form of fine art that uses live performers to present the experience of a real or imagined event before a live audience in a specific place.
