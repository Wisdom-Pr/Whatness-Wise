Learning outcomes

Education
Also: learning objectives

Learning outcomes are measurable statements that define the specific knowledge, skills, or attitudes a student must demonstrate to verify the successful completion of an instructional module.

Category
Educational objective
Identity
A measurable statement of student competence
Mechanism
Defining the specific knowledge, skills, or attitudes acquired through instruction
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish between the 'process' of teaching and the 'result' of learning, and frequently omits the necessity of assessment criteria.

Usually defined as: Statements that describe what a student will be able to do at the end of a course.
