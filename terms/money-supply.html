Money supply

Economics

Money supply is the total stock of currency and liquid deposits held by the public in an economy that is available for immediate use in transactions at a specific point in time.

Category
Economic indicator
Identity
The total stock of liquid assets available for exchange within an economy at a specific time.
Mechanism
Aggregation of currency, demand deposits, and other liquid equivalents held by the public and non-bank entities.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between the stock of money and the flow of transactions, and it often omits the specific distinction between base money and broader liquidity measures.

Usually defined as: The total amount of money in circulation in an economy.
