Ode

Literature

An ode is a formal lyric poem that celebrates or exalts a specific subject through the use of elevated language and a sustained, complex stanzaic structure.

Category
Literary Genre
Identity
A structured lyric poem composed as a formal address to a specific subject, person, or event.
Mechanism
The use of elevated language, complex stanzaic patterns, and sustained emotional intensity to exalt or celebrate its subject.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish odes from other lyric forms like elegies or hymns, which also use elevated address; it lacks the requirement of a formal, sustained structure.

Usually defined as: A lyric poem in the form of an address to a particular subject, often elevated in style or manner.
