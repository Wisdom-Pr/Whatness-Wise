Psychological trauma

Mental Health
Also: trauma

Psychological trauma is a persistent state of cognitive and emotional dysregulation produced when an overwhelming experience exceeds an individual's capacity to integrate the event into their existing mental framework.

Category
Psychological state
Identity
A persistent alteration in cognitive and emotional processing
Mechanism
The failure of the brain's integration systems to process an overwhelming experience, resulting in the continued re-experiencing of the event as a present threat.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'trauma' to describe the 'response' and fails to distinguish trauma from simple stress or grief by omitting the mechanism of failed integration.

Usually defined as: Psychological trauma is an emotional response to a distressing event that overwhelms an individual's ability to cope.
