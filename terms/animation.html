Animation

Film Studies

Animation is a visual technique that creates the illusion of movement by displaying a sequence of static images in rapid succession, relying on the human eye's inability to perceive individual frames at high speeds.

Category
Visual technique
Identity
A sequence of static images displayed in rapid succession to simulate movement.
Mechanism
Persistence of vision and the phi phenomenon.
Resolved
The standard definition often omits the distinction between traditional frame-by-frame rendering and procedural generation, and fails to distinguish animation from video recording.

Usually defined as: The process of making the illusion of motion and change by means of the rapid display of a sequence of images that minimally differ from each other.
