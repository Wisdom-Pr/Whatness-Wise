Addiction

Mental Health
Also: addictive disorder

Addiction is a neurobehavioral condition characterized by the compulsive engagement in a rewarding stimulus despite harmful consequences, driven by a persistent dysregulation of the brain's reinforcement and inhibitory control systems.

Category
Neurobehavioral condition
Identity
A pathological state of the brain's reward and executive function systems
Mechanism
persistent dysregulation of dopamine-mediated reinforcement pathways that overrides inhibitory control
Resolved
The standard definition focuses only on substance use, failing to account for behavioral addictions (e.g., gambling) and the biological distinction between physical dependence and psychological compulsion.

Usually defined as: A chronic, relapsing disorder characterized by compulsive drug seeking and use despite adverse consequences.
