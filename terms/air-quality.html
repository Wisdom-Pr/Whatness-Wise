Air quality

Environmental Health

Air quality is the measured concentration of specific gaseous and particulate pollutants in a volume of ambient atmosphere relative to established health and ecological safety thresholds.

Category
Environmental metric
Identity
The measured concentration of specific gaseous and particulate pollutants within a given volume of ambient atmosphere.
Mechanism
Comparison of observed substance levels against established health and ecological safety thresholds.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular and vague, failing to distinguish 'quality' from the mere presence of pollutants or to account for the threshold-based nature of the measurement.

Usually defined as: The degree to which the air in a particular place is pollution-free.
