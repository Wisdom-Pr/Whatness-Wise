Community (sociology)

Sociology / Communication
Also: community

Community is a social unit composed of individuals who share a common identity, norms, and collective interaction that foster a sense of belonging and mutual interdependence.

Category
Social structure
Identity
A social unit characterized by shared identity and interaction
Mechanism
Interdependence and social cohesion
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it could describe any demographic group or collection of people without implying the necessary social bonds or shared norms that distinguish a community from a mere aggregate.

Usually defined as: A group of people living in the same place or having a particular characteristic in common.
