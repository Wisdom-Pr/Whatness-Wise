Heritage conservation

Geography
Also: historic preservation

Heritage conservation is the professional practice of managing change to cultural assets through maintenance, restoration, or legal protection to ensure their historical significance and material authenticity are preserved for future generations.

Category
Professional practice
Identity
Systematic intervention to maintain the integrity of cultural assets
Mechanism
Applying maintenance, restoration, and legal protection to preserve historical significance and material authenticity
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between mere maintenance and the specific goal of retaining 'authenticity' and 'significance' which are the defining constraints of the field.

Usually defined as: The practice of preserving and protecting historical buildings, sites, and cultural artifacts from decay or destruction.
