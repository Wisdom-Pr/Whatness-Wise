Electricity

Physics

Electricity is the physical phenomenon arising from the existence and interaction of electric charge, which manifests as either stationary fields or the movement of charged particles through a medium.

Category
Physical phenomenon
Identity
The presence and motion of electric charge.
Mechanism
The interaction of electromagnetic fields and the displacement of subatomic particles within a medium or vacuum.
Resolved
The standard definition is too narrow; it describes current (the flow) rather than the phenomenon itself, and it ignores static electricity where no flow occurs.

Usually defined as: The flow of electrons through a conductor.
