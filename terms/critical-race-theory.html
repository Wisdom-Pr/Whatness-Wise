Critical race theory

Critical Theory
Also: CRT

Critical race theory is an academic framework that examines how legal systems and societal structures maintain racial inequality by analyzing the historical and systemic nature of power.

Category
Academic framework
Identity
A theoretical lens for analyzing the intersection of law, power, and racial inequality.
Mechanism
Critiquing how neutral-appearing legal structures and policies perpetuate historical racial disparities.
Resolved
Common definitions often conflate the framework with its political applications or fail to distinguish it from general sociological studies of race.

Usually defined as: A framework that examines how systemic racism is embedded in legal systems and societal structures.
