Environmental health

Environmental Health

Environmental health is the branch of public health that manages the impact of physical, chemical, and biological factors in the natural and built environment on human well-being through the identification and control of environmental hazards.

Category
Public health discipline
Identity
The intersection of environmental factors and human health outcomes
Mechanism
Identifying, evaluating, and controlling physical, chemical, and biological stressors in the surroundings
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, failing to distinguish the field from general environmental science or clinical medicine by omitting the focus on human population risk management.

Usually defined as: The branch of public health that is concerned with all aspects of the natural and built environment that may affect human health.
