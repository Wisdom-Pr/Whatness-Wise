Dividend

Finance

Dividend is the portion of a corporation's net earnings distributed to shareholders as authorized by the board of directors, representing a return on their equity investment.

Category
Financial distribution
Identity
A portion of corporate earnings allocated to equity holders
Mechanism
Formal declaration by a board of directors to distribute liquid assets or stock
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish dividends from other corporate payouts like share buybacks or interest payments, and it implies that all profits are distributed, which is inaccurate.

Usually defined as: A sum of money paid regularly by a company to its shareholders out of its profits.
