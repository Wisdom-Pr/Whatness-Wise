Gene therapy

Pharmacology

Gene therapy is a medical intervention that modifies the genetic material of a patient's cells to treat or prevent disease by introducing, removing, or altering specific DNA sequences.

Category
Medical intervention
Identity
A technique that modifies the genetic material of living cells to achieve a therapeutic effect.
Mechanism
The delivery of functional genetic sequences into target cells to replace, silence, or supplement malfunctioning or absent genes.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish gene therapy from basic genetic research or diagnostic genetic testing, and it implies the process only involves 'inserting' genes, ignoring silencing or editing mechanisms.

Usually defined as: The use of genes to treat or prevent disease by inserting a gene into a patient's cells instead of using drugs or surgery.
