Lawsuit

Law

A lawsuit is a formal dispute resolution process initiated by a plaintiff in a court of law to obtain a judicial remedy for a claimed violation of rights or breach of duty.

Category
Legal proceeding
Identity
A formal dispute resolution process
Mechanism
The filing of a claim by a plaintiff seeking a judicial remedy for a perceived wrong or breach of obligation
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'legal' to define 'lawsuit' and fails to distinguish between the act of filing and the resulting judicial process.

Usually defined as: A legal action brought against another person or entity in a court of law.
