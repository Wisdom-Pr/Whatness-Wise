Wisdom

General Knowledge

Wisdom is the cognitive capacity to synthesize accumulated knowledge and experience into sound judgment that aligns actions with long-term ethical and practical consequences.

Category
Cognitive faculty
Identity
The integration of knowledge, experience, and deep understanding to guide decision-making.
Mechanism
Synthesizing disparate information with ethical foresight to discern optimal outcomes.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular and vague, as it uses 'good judgments'—which itself requires wisdom to define—and fails to distinguish wisdom from mere intelligence or expertise.

Usually defined as: The ability to use knowledge and experience to make good judgments.
