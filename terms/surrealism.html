Surrealism

Visual Arts

Surrealism is an artistic and literary movement that seeks to reveal the hidden truths of the unconscious mind by bypassing rational control through the use of dreamlike imagery and spontaneous, non-logical associations.

Category
Cultural and artistic movement
Identity
A movement prioritizing the expression of the unconscious mind over rational thought.
Mechanism
The systematic use of dreamlike imagery, bizarre juxtapositions, and automatic techniques to bypass logical control.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish Surrealism from Dadaism, which also utilized irrationality but lacked the specific focus on psychoanalytic theory and the unconscious mind.

Usually defined as: An artistic and literary movement that sought to release the creative potential of the unconscious mind by the irrational juxtaposition of images.
