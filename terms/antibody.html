Antibody

Medicine

Antibody is a specialized protein produced by the immune system that identifies and neutralizes foreign substances by binding to their unique molecular markers.

Category
Glycoprotein
Identity
An immunoglobulin molecule synthesized by plasma cells.
Mechanism
Binding specifically to a unique molecular site on a foreign pathogen to neutralize it or mark it for destruction.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish antibodies from other immune proteins like cytokines or complement proteins, and it omits the structural specificity that defines their function.

Usually defined as: A blood protein produced in response to and counteracting a specific antigen.
