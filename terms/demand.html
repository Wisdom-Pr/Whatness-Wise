Demand

Economics

Demand is the functional relationship between the price of a good or service and the quantity that consumers are willing and able to purchase at each price point.

Category
Economic quantity
Identity
The functional relationship between price and quantity desired
Mechanism
The interaction of consumer preference, budget constraint, and market price
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates 'demand' with 'quantity demanded' (a specific point on a curve) and fails to explicitly state the inverse relationship required to distinguish it from a mere 'want' or 'need'.

Usually defined as: The amount of a good or service that consumers are willing and able to purchase at various prices.
