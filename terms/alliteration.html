Alliteration

Literature

Alliteration is the repetition of identical initial consonant sounds in successive or closely positioned words that serves to create auditory rhythm or emphasize specific linguistic patterns.

Category
Literary device
Identity
Repetition of initial consonant sounds in a sequence of words
Mechanism
Phonetic alignment of stressed syllables to create rhythmic or auditory emphasis
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish alliteration from assonance (vowel repetition) or general repetition, and incorrectly implies it applies to any 'letter' rather than specifically to the sound produced.

Usually defined as: The occurrence of the same letter or sound at the beginning of adjacent or closely connected words.
