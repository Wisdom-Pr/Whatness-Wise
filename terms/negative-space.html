Negative space

Visual Arts

Negative space is the unoccupied area surrounding and between the main subjects of a visual composition that actively defines their boundaries and balances the overall design.

Category
spatial visual element
Identity
the unoccupied area surrounding and between the main subjects of a visual composition
Mechanism
actively defining the boundaries of those subjects and balancing the overall visual layout
Resolved
Standard definitions treat it passively as mere empty space, missing its active role in shaping the main subject's form and maintaining visual balance.

Usually defined as: The empty space around and between the subject or subjects of an image.
