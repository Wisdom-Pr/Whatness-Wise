Concrete

Construction and Materials

Concrete is a composite construction material formed by the chemical hydration of a binder that binds inert mineral aggregates into a rigid, load-bearing structural mass.

Category
Composite material
Identity
A synthetic stone formed by the chemical hydration of a binder and the mechanical binding of aggregates.
Mechanism
The chemical reaction between water and a cementitious binder that creates a crystalline matrix, locking inert mineral fillers into a rigid, load-bearing mass.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish concrete from mortar (which lacks coarse aggregate) and fails to identify the chemical hydration process that differentiates it from simple physical mixtures.

Usually defined as: A hard, strong building material made by mixing a cementing material with sand and gravel.
