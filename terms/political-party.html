Political party

Public Administration

A political party is a formal organization that seeks to influence public policy and exercise government authority by nominating candidates for elective office and coordinating their actions within the legislative process.

Category
Political organization
Identity
A coalition of individuals sharing common policy preferences and ideological goals.
Mechanism
Mobilizing voters and coordinating legislative action to secure control of government institutions.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish parties from interest groups or social movements, which also share ideologies and influence policy but do not necessarily seek to occupy government offices through electoral competition.

Usually defined as: An organized group of people who have the same ideology and work to get their candidates elected to office.
