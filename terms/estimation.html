Estimation

Mathematics / Statistics

Estimation is the analytical process of calculating an approximate value for an unknown quantity by applying systematic mathematical or statistical methods to observed data.

Category
Analytical process
Identity
The approximation of an unknown parameter or quantity based on available data or incomplete information.
Mechanism
Applying statistical models or heuristic reasoning to reduce uncertainty regarding a target value.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'guess' and fails to distinguish estimation from mere guessing or speculation, which lacks the systematic methodology of estimation.

Usually defined as: The process of making an educated guess about a value or quantity.
