Saturated fat

Nutrition

Saturated fat is a type of lipid molecule containing fatty acid chains with no double bonds between carbon atoms, allowing the chains to pack together in a straight, dense structure.

Category
Lipid
Identity
A triglyceride molecule containing fatty acids with no double bonds between carbon atoms.
Mechanism
The absence of carbon-carbon double bonds allows the hydrocarbon chains to pack tightly together in a straight, linear configuration.
Resolved
The standard definition focuses on the chemical bond type without explaining the structural consequence (linear packing) that distinguishes it from unsaturated fats.

Usually defined as: A type of fat in which the fatty acid chains have all or predominantly single bonds.
