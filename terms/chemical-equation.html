Chemical equation

Chemistry

A chemical equation is a symbolic representation of a chemical reaction that uses formulas and stoichiometric coefficients to show the transformation of reactants into products while maintaining the conservation of mass.

Category
Symbolic representation
Identity
A concise notation depicting the transformation of reactants into products.
Mechanism
The use of chemical formulas and stoichiometric coefficients to satisfy the law of conservation of mass.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to mention the conservation of mass, which is the unique constraint that distinguishes a valid chemical equation from a mere list of reactants and products.

Usually defined as: A symbolic representation of a chemical reaction using formulas and symbols.
