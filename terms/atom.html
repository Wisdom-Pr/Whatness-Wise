Atom

Chemistry

An atom is the smallest constituent unit of matter that retains the chemical properties of a specific element, consisting of a central nucleus surrounded by a cloud of electrons.

Category
Fundamental particle structure
Identity
The smallest constituent unit of matter that retains the chemical properties of an element.
Mechanism
A dense central nucleus of protons and neutrons surrounded by a cloud of negatively charged electrons held together by electromagnetic forces.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish atoms from subatomic particles like protons or electrons, which are also 'units' of matter but do not retain the chemical properties of an element.

Usually defined as: The smallest unit of ordinary matter that forms a chemical element.
