Infant mortality

Reproductive and Family Health

Infant mortality is a statistical rate representing the number of deaths of children before their first birthday per 1,000 live births in a given population.

Category
Statistical rate
Identity
A demographic measure of death frequency within a specific age cohort.
Mechanism
The ratio of deaths of children under one year to the total number of live births in a given population and time period.
Resolved
The standard definition often omits the necessary normalization factor (per 1,000 live births), which is required to distinguish it from a raw count of deaths.

Usually defined as: The number of deaths of infants under one year of age per 1,000 live births.
