Ge'ez

Linguistics
Also: Geez, Ethiopic

Ge'ez is an extinct South Semitic language that serves as the liturgical and literary foundation for Ethiopian culture, preserved primarily through its distinct, original abugida writing system.

Category
Language
Identity
A liturgical and literary language of the Ethiopic branch of Semitic languages.
Mechanism
Utilizes a unique abugida script to record religious and legal texts, serving as the historical root for modern Ethiopian languages.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish it from other Semitic languages by omitting its unique script and its specific role as a non-spoken, preserved liturgical language.

Usually defined as: An ancient South Semitic language of Ethiopia.
