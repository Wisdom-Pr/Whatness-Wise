Theme

Literature

Theme is a unifying core insight about human experience that a creative work conveys through its recurring elements, characters, and plot.

Category
unifying core insight
Identity
an abstract statement about human experience
Mechanism
conveyed through a narrative's characters, plot, and motifs
Resolved
Fails discrimination because defining theme as simply the 'main idea' causes students to confuse it with the story's concrete topic or plot summary.

Usually defined as: The underlying main idea or message of a literary work.
