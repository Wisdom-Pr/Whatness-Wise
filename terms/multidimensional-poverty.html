Multidimensional poverty

Social Welfare

Multidimensional poverty is a composite measure of deprivation that identifies the proportion of a population experiencing simultaneous deficits in health, education, and living standards by aggregating weighted indicators of essential human development.

Category
Socioeconomic metric
Identity
A composite index of overlapping deprivations experienced by individuals.
Mechanism
Aggregating weighted indicators of non-monetary deficits to identify the proportion of the population lacking essential human development requirements.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish between the index itself and the raw data, and it lacks a clear mechanism for how the 'multidimensional' aspect is calculated versus simple poverty.

Usually defined as: A measure of poverty that considers multiple deprivations beyond just income, such as health, education, and living standards.
