Family planning

Reproductive and Family Health

Family planning is the deliberate management of the number and timing of children through the use of contraceptive methods or fertility regulation to align reproductive outcomes with individual or household goals.

Category
Public health practice
Identity
The deliberate management of reproductive outcomes through the timing and spacing of pregnancies.
Mechanism
The application of contraceptive methods, medical counseling, or fertility awareness to align reproductive capacity with personal or socioeconomic goals.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the practice (the act) with the methods (the tools), failing to distinguish it from mere birth control, which can be involuntary or singular in focus.

Usually defined as: The practice of controlling the number of children in a family and the intervals between their births, particularly by means of artificial contraception or voluntary sterilization.
