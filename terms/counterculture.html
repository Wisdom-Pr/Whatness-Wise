Counterculture

Anthropology

Counterculture is a social movement that challenges the dominant values and institutional structures of a society by actively promoting and practicing alternative lifestyles and belief systems.

Category
Social phenomenon
Identity
A subculture whose values and norms of behavior differ substantially from those of mainstream society.
Mechanism
The systematic rejection of established social conventions and institutional power structures in favor of alternative lifestyles and ideologies.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'norm' to define a group defined by its opposition to norms, and it fails to distinguish counterculture from mere subculture, which may coexist with mainstream society rather than actively opposing it.

Usually defined as: A way of life and set of attitudes opposed to or at variance with the prevailing social norm.
