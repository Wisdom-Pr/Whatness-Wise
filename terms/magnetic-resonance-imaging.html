Magnetic resonance imaging

Medicine
Also: MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging is a medical diagnostic technique that produces detailed images of internal body structures by detecting the radio frequency signals emitted by hydrogen nuclei as they return to equilibrium after being perturbed by a strong magnetic field.

Category
Diagnostic imaging modality
Identity
Non-invasive visualization of internal anatomical structures
Mechanism
Detection of radio frequency signals emitted by hydrogen nuclei excited by magnetic fields
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish MRI from other modalities like CT or ultrasound by omitting the reliance on nuclear magnetic resonance of protons.

Usually defined as: A medical imaging technique that uses magnetic fields and radio waves to create images of the organs in the body.
