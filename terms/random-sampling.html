Random sampling

Statistics

Random sampling is a statistical selection technique in which every member of a target population has a known, non-zero probability of being chosen, ensuring unbiased estimates of the whole group.

Category
Data collection method
Identity
a statistical selection technique
Mechanism
assigning every member of a population a known, non-zero probability of being chosen
Resolved
Confuses general random sampling with simple random sampling by requiring equal probability rather than merely known, non-zero probability.

Usually defined as: A sampling technique where every member of a population has an equal chance of being selected.
