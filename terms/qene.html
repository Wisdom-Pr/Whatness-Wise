Qene

Literature
Also: Qine, Ethiopian poetry

Qene is a poetic composition that conveys a primary literal meaning alongside a secondary, hidden meaning to reveal a deeper truth.

Category
Literary form
Identity
A poetic composition utilizing semantic ambiguity
Mechanism
The systematic layering of a literal meaning over a hidden, figurative meaning
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish the mechanism of 'wax and gold' (the double meaning) from general metaphor or allegory.

Usually defined as: Qene is a term used in Ge'ez and Amharic to refer to a specific form of Ethiopian poetic composition characterized by double meanings.
