SDAC

Computer Science and Technology
Also: Structurizer and Definer of Academic Concepts, Sidak

SDAC (Structurizer and Definer of Academic Concepts) is a software application that defines an academic concept by structurizing it into its Category, Identity, and Mechanism and resolving these into one complete definition.

Category
software application
Identity
structurizer and definer of academic concepts
Mechanism
structurizing an academic concept into its Category, Identity, and Mechanism and resolving them into one complete definition
Resolved
Dictionaries and glossaries list what a term is usually taken to mean; an SDAC instead builds the definition from the concept's own structure, so it states what the concept is, what makes it distinct, and how it works. WiseDefine is an SDAC application.
