Anthropology

Anthropology is the systematic study of humanity that integrates biological, cultural, linguistic, and archaeological evidence to understand the full scope of human experience across time and space.

Category
Academic discipline
Identity
Systematic study of humanity
Mechanism
Comparative analysis of cultural, biological, linguistic, and archaeological evidence
Resolved
The standard definition is overly broad and fails to distinguish the field from sociology or history, which also study human societies.

Usually defined as: The study of human societies and cultures and their development.
