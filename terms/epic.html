Epic

Literature

Epic is a narrative genre of grand scale and elevated style that centers on a protagonist whose actions determine the fate of a culture or civilization.

Category
Literary genre
Identity
A narrative work of grand scale and elevated style
Mechanism
The depiction of a hero whose actions determine the fate of a nation or culture
Resolved
The standard definition focuses too heavily on poetry and oral tradition, failing to account for modern prose epics and cinematic narratives.

Usually defined as: A long poem, typically one derived from ancient oral tradition, narrating the deeds and adventures of heroic or legendary figures.
