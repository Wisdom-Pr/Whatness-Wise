Proverb

Literature

A proverb is a traditional, culturally established expression that conveys a widely accepted truth or practical advice through a concise, fixed metaphorical or literal statement.

Category
Linguistic expression
Identity
A traditional, culturally transmitted statement of wisdom or experience.
Mechanism
The use of metaphorical or literal imagery to summarize complex social observations into a memorable, fixed structure.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish proverbs from aphorisms or idioms; it lacks the requirement of traditional, communal transmission and fixed, recognizable form.

Usually defined as: A short, well-known pithy saying, stating a general truth or piece of advice.
