Cardiology

Medicine

Cardiology is the branch of medicine focused on the diagnosis and treatment of disorders affecting the heart and the blood vessel network.

Category
Medical specialty
Identity
The study and clinical practice focused on the cardiovascular system
Mechanism
Diagnosis, medical management, and surgical intervention of heart and blood vessel disorders
Resolved
The standard definition fails to explicitly include the blood vessels, which are integral to the cardiovascular system, and implies only a focus on disease rather than the maintenance of healthy function.

Usually defined as: The branch of medicine that deals with diseases and abnormalities of the heart.
