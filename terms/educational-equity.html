Educational equity

Education

Educational equity is the distribution of academic resources and support based on the specific needs of individual students to ensure that personal or social circumstances do not prevent them from achieving their full potential.

Category
Social construct
Identity
A principle of resource distribution and policy design
Mechanism
Allocating support based on individual student needs rather than uniform provision
Resolved
The standard definition fails Rule 5 (discrimination) by confusing equity with equality, and fails Rule 2 (condition) by implying equity is a practice rather than a distributive outcome.

Usually defined as: The practice of ensuring all students have access to the same opportunities and resources to succeed in school.
