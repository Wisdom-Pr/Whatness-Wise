Passive voice

English Language

Passive voice is a grammatical construction where the subject of a clause undergoes the action expressed by the verb, achieved by promoting the patient to the subject position and optionally demoting or omitting the agent.

Category
Grammatical voice
Identity
A syntactic arrangement of a clause
Mechanism
The focus is shifted from the agent to the patient by making the object of the action the grammatical subject and using a form of the verb 'to be' with a past participle.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to mention the optionality of the agent and the structural requirement of the auxiliary verb, and it relies on the vague term 'action'.

Usually defined as: A grammatical construction where the subject receives the action of the verb rather than performing it.
