Market opportunity

Entrepreneurship

Market opportunity is a potential for commercial gain that arises when a specific group of customers has an unmet need that a firm can satisfy through a viable product or service.

Category
Economic condition
Identity
A potential for commercial gain within a specific customer segment.
Mechanism
The alignment of unmet consumer needs with a firm's ability to provide a viable solution.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular because it defines 'opportunity' as a 'situation' without specifying the relationship between demand and supply, and it fails to distinguish opportunity from a simple 'market' or 'niche'.

Usually defined as: A situation where a business can sell a product or service to a specific group of people to make a profit.
