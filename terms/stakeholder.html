Stakeholder

Business

Stakeholder is an individual or group that holds a legitimate interest in an organization because they can influence, or are impacted by, the organization's strategic objectives and operational results.

Category
Entity
Identity
Any individual or group that can affect or is affected by the objectives and actions of an organization.
Mechanism
The existence of a mutual dependency or claim between the entity and the organization's outcomes.
Resolved
The standard definition is overly broad, as it fails to distinguish between those who have a legal or contractual claim and those who are merely interested observers.

Usually defined as: A person or group that has an interest or concern in an organization.
