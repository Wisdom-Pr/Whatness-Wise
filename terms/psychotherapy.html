Psychotherapy

Mental Health

Psychotherapy is a structured interpersonal process that facilitates change in a person's thoughts, feelings, and behaviors through the application of evidence-based clinical techniques.

Category
Clinical intervention
Identity
A structured interpersonal process
Mechanism
The application of psychological principles and techniques to modify maladaptive behavior, cognition, and emotional patterns
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'psychological' to define 'psychotherapy' and fails to distinguish it from informal counseling or self-help by omitting the requirement of a professional, evidence-based framework.

Usually defined as: The treatment of mental disorders by psychological rather than medical means.
