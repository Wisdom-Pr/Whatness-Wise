Fact

General Knowledge

Fact is an objective occurrence or condition in reality that is established through empirical observation or logical demonstration.

Category
Epistemic state
Identity
An objective occurrence or condition in reality
Mechanism
verification through empirical observation or logical demonstration
Resolved
The standard definition is circular because 'true' is the adjective form of 'truth,' which is synonymous with 'fact' in many contexts, and it fails to distinguish facts from opinions or theories.

Usually defined as: A thing that is known or proved to be true.
