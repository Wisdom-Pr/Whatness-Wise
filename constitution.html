Constitution

Social Science / Law

A constitution is a state's foundational law, defining and limiting government power and setting the standard all other laws must meet.

CategoryA state's foundational law
IdentityDefines and limits government power
MechanismSets the standard all other laws must meet
ResolvedConventional phrasing ("principles by which a state is governed") drops the limiting-government function that discriminates it from a charter or legal code; "all... all" and "measure" were tightened for concision
