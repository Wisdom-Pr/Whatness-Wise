Architectural design

Construction and Materials

Architectural design is the systematic process of configuring the form, function, and spatial arrangement of a structure to satisfy human requirements within the constraints of site, budget, and technical building standards.

Category
Professional practice
Identity
A systematic process of conceptualizing and detailing the form, function, and spatial arrangement of a built environment.
Mechanism
The integration of aesthetic intent, structural requirements, and human utility within the constraints of site, budget, and building codes.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish design from the broader act of construction and fails to specify the unique constraint-based nature of the discipline.

Usually defined as: The process of planning, designing, and constructing buildings or other structures.
