Supply chain management

Operations Management

Supply chain management is the systemic coordination of the flow of goods, services, information, and finances from point of origin to point of consumption, exercised through the integration of sourcing, manufacturing, and distribution processes to maximize customer value.

Category
Management discipline
Identity
The systemic coordination of the flow of goods, services, information, and finances across the entire value network.
Mechanism
Optimizing the integration of sourcing, manufacturing, and distribution processes to maximize customer value and achieve a sustainable competitive advantage.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the 'supply chain' (the network itself) with 'management' (the optimization of that network) and fails to explicitly include the flow of information and finances.

Usually defined as: The management of the flow of goods and services including all processes that transform raw materials into final products.
