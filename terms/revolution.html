Revolution

History

Revolution is the complete displacement of a preceding political or physical state by a new structure or position, achieved either through the collective overthrow of an established government or the full orbital transit of a body around a central point.

Category
Process
Identity
A fundamental change in political authority or a complete orbital cycle.
Mechanism
The displacement of an existing power structure by a collective movement or the physical rotation of a body around an external axis.
Resolved
The standard definition conflates two unrelated phenomena (political upheaval and orbital mechanics) without identifying the commonality of 'complete displacement' or 'return to a starting state' that distinguishes them from mere change or rotation.

Usually defined as: A forcible overthrow of a government or social order in favor of a new system, or the movement of an object in a circular course around a central point.
