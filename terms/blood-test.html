Blood test

Medicine

A blood test is a diagnostic procedure that evaluates health by measuring the concentration or presence of specific chemical, cellular, or biological components within a sampled volume of blood.

Category
Diagnostic procedure
Identity
Analysis of biological fluid composition
Mechanism
Quantitative or qualitative measurement of specific analytes, cells, or pathogens within a peripheral blood sample
Resolved
The standard definition is overly broad, failing to distinguish a blood test from other diagnostic procedures (like imaging) and failing to specify that it relies on the chemical or cellular composition of the blood rather than just the act of taking it.

Usually defined as: A medical procedure where a sample of blood is taken to check for diseases or health conditions.
