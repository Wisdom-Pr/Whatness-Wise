Inflammation

Medicine

Inflammation is a protective biological response triggered by tissue damage or pathogens that recruits immune cells and proteins to the affected site to eliminate the cause of injury and initiate tissue repair.

Category
Biological response
Identity
A protective immune mechanism
Mechanism
The recruitment of leukocytes and plasma proteins to a site of tissue damage or pathogen invasion
Resolved
The standard definition lists symptoms (redness, heat) rather than the underlying biological mechanism, and fails to distinguish inflammation from a simple immune reaction.

Usually defined as: The body's response to injury or infection, characterized by redness, heat, swelling, and pain.
