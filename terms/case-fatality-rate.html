Case fatality rate

Epidemiology

Case fatality rate is the proportion of individuals who die from a specific disease, calculated by dividing the number of deaths from that disease by the total number of confirmed cases.

Category
Ratio
Identity
A measure of disease severity
Mechanism
The quotient of total deaths attributed to a specific condition divided by the total number of confirmed cases of that condition within a defined population and timeframe.
Resolved
Common definitions often fail to specify that the numerator must be a subset of the denominator, leading to potential confusion with mortality rates which use the entire population as the denominator.

Usually defined as: The proportion of people who die from a specified disease among all individuals diagnosed with the disease.
