World Heritage Site

Geography
Also: UNESCO World Heritage

A World Heritage Site is a landmark or area legally protected by international treaty because it possesses outstanding universal value to humanity.

Category
Designated site
Identity
A location recognized by the international community for possessing outstanding universal value to humanity.
Mechanism
Inclusion on the World Heritage List under the 1972 UNESCO Convention.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the site itself with the act of listing, failing to distinguish it from national landmarks that lack international treaty-based protection.

Usually defined as: A landmark or area with legal protection by an international convention administered by UNESCO because of its cultural, historical, scientific, or other form of significance.
