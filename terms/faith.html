Faith

Religious Studies

Faith is a firm conviction held in the absence of empirical evidence, exercised through the commitment of the will to a belief or authority.

Category
Epistemic stance
Identity
A conviction held in the absence of empirical evidence or logical demonstration.
Mechanism
The commitment of the intellect and will to a proposition or entity based on internal trust or authority rather than external verification.
Resolved
The standard definition fails Rule 5 because it could describe 'guesswork' or 'delusion'; it also fails Rule 2 by confusing the condition (lack of proof) with the identity (the act of commitment).

Usually defined as: Belief in something without proof.
