Chiropractic

Pharmacology
Also: chiropractor

Chiropractic is a healthcare practice focused on the diagnosis and manual treatment of musculoskeletal disorders, exercised through the controlled adjustment of the spinal column to restore joint mobility and optimize nervous system function.

Category
Healthcare practice
Identity
A system of physical manipulation and manual adjustment of the spinal column and joints.
Mechanism
Applying controlled force to specific vertebrae to restore joint mobility and alleviate nerve interference.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish chiropractic from physical therapy or osteopathy, which also utilize manual manipulation, and it often relies on the vague term 'alternative medicine' rather than the specific mechanical focus.

Usually defined as: A form of alternative medicine concerned with the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of mechanical disorders of the musculoskeletal system, especially of the spine.
