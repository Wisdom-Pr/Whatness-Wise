Research question

Research Methods

A research question is a focused, answerable inquiry that defines the specific scope and objective of an investigation to guide the systematic collection and analysis of evidence.

Category
Inquiry framework
Identity
A focused, interrogative statement defining the scope and objective of an investigation.
Mechanism
Narrowing a broad topic into a specific, answerable problem that guides data collection and analysis.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular, as it defines the term using the word 'research' itself, and fails to distinguish a research question from a general inquiry or a simple factual query.

Usually defined as: A question that a research project sets out to answer.
