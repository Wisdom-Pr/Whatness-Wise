Communism

Political Science

Communism is a socio-economic system of collective ownership that eliminates private property and market exchange, distributing all produced goods based on individual need.

Category
Socio-economic system
Identity
A system of social organization based on the collective ownership of the means of production.
Mechanism
The abolition of private property and the distribution of goods based on individual need rather than market exchange.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish communism from socialism or communalism, as it lacks the specific requirement of the total elimination of private property and the state apparatus.

Usually defined as: A social organization where all property is owned by the community and each person contributes and receives according to their ability and needs.
