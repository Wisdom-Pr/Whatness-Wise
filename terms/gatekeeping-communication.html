Gatekeeping (communication)

Media and Communication
Also: gatekeeping

Gatekeeping is the process of controlling the flow of information through a communication channel by applying selective criteria that determine which content reaches an audience and which is excluded.

Category
Social process
Identity
The selective control of information flow
Mechanism
The application of editorial or algorithmic criteria to determine which content is included or excluded from a public or private channel
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish gatekeeping from simple censorship or curation, and often ignores the role of modern algorithmic automation in non-human contexts.

Usually defined as: The process through which information is filtered for dissemination, whether for publication, broadcasting, the internet, or some other mode of communication.
