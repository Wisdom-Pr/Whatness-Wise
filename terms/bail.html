Bail

Law

Bail is a financial or property-based security provided to the court to obtain the temporary release of an accused person, which is forfeited if the individual fails to appear for required legal proceedings.

Category
Legal instrument
Identity
A financial or property-based guarantee provided to the court.
Mechanism
The conditional release of an accused person from custody in exchange for a security deposit that is forfeited if they fail to appear for scheduled legal proceedings.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish bail from a fine or a bond, and often omits the condition of forfeiture upon non-appearance.

Usually defined as: Money paid to a court to ensure a defendant returns for trial.
