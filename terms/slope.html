Slope

Mathematics

Slope is the numerical measure of a line's steepness and direction, calculated as the ratio of the change in vertical coordinates to the corresponding change in horizontal coordinates between any two points on that line.

Category
Geometric property
Identity
The measure of the steepness and direction of a line.
Mechanism
The ratio of the vertical change to the horizontal change between two distinct points on a line.
Resolved
The standard 'rise over run' is a mnemonic, not a definition, and fails to specify that it is a ratio of coordinate differences.

Usually defined as: The rise over run of a line.
