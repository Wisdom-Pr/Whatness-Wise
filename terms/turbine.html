Turbine

Mechanical Engineering

A turbine is a rotary mechanical device that extracts kinetic energy from a moving fluid stream to produce rotational torque by forcing the fluid to do work against a series of blades.

Category
Energy conversion device
Identity
A rotary mechanical device that extracts kinetic energy from a fluid stream.
Mechanism
The interaction between fluid momentum and rotor blades that converts fluid flow into rotational torque.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a turbine from a propeller or a fan, which also interact with fluid flow but consume energy to move the fluid rather than extracting energy from it.

Usually defined as: A machine for producing continuous power in which a wheel or rotor is made to revolve by a fast-moving flow of water, steam, gas, or air.
