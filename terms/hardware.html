Hardware

Computer Science

Hardware is the collection of tangible electronic and mechanical components of a computing system that execute instructions and process data through the manipulation of physical states.

Category
Physical technology
Identity
The collection of tangible, electronic, and mechanical components of a computing system.
Mechanism
Execution of instructions through the manipulation of electrical signals and physical states.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish hardware from general physical objects or laboratory equipment by omitting its functional relationship to software instructions.

Usually defined as: The physical components of a computer system.
