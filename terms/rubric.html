Rubric

Education
Also: assessment rubric

Rubric is an evaluation instrument that defines specific criteria and performance levels to guide the consistent assessment of a task or product.

Category
Evaluation instrument
Identity
A structured set of criteria and performance levels used to assess the quality of a specific task or product.
Mechanism
Mapping defined performance expectations against observed evidence to produce a consistent score or qualitative judgment.
Resolved
Common definitions often fail to distinguish a rubric from a simple checklist or a generic grading scale, missing the requirement of defined performance levels.

Usually defined as: A guide listing specific criteria for grading or scoring academic work.
