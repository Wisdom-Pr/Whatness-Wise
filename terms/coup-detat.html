Coup d'état

Political Science
Also: coup, coup d'etat

A coup d'état is the abrupt, extra-constitutional seizure of state power by a small group of internal actors who utilize the government's own administrative or military apparatus to replace the existing leadership.

Category
Political action
Identity
The abrupt, extra-constitutional removal of an existing government's leadership.
Mechanism
The subversion of the state's existing administrative or military apparatus by internal actors.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a coup from a revolution or civil war, as it lacks the requirement that the change occurs within the existing state structure rather than through a mass uprising.

Usually defined as: A sudden, violent, and illegal seizure of power from a government by a small group.
