Free trade

Economics

Free trade is an economic policy that enables the unrestricted exchange of goods and services between countries by eliminating government-imposed barriers and discriminatory practices that favor domestic producers over foreign competitors.

Category
Economic policy
Identity
A system of international exchange
Mechanism
The removal of government-imposed barriers such as tariffs, quotas, and subsidies on the movement of goods and services between countries.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish free trade from simple deregulation by omitting the core requirement of non-discrimination between domestic and foreign producers.

Usually defined as: A policy where governments do not discriminate against imports or interfere with exports by applying tariffs or quotas.
