Novel

Literature

A novel is a sustained, complex work of prose fiction that develops multiple interconnected characters and subplots within an extended narrative structure.

Category
Literary form
Identity
A sustained, complex narrative work of prose fiction.
Mechanism
The development of multiple interconnected characters and subplots within a cohesive, extended structure.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a novel from a novella or short story, as it lacks a reference to length or structural complexity.

Usually defined as: A long, fictional prose narrative.
