Political philosophy

Political Science

Political philosophy is the systematic inquiry into the moral legitimacy and foundational principles of collective governance, determining how power and rights ought to be structured within a society.

Category
Academic discipline
Identity
The systematic inquiry into the moral and structural legitimacy of collective human governance.
Mechanism
The application of ethical frameworks and logic to evaluate the origins, scope, and justification of power and rights within a society.
Resolved
The standard definition is a list of topics rather than a definition, failing to distinguish the field from political science (which describes how systems function) or political history (which records events).

Usually defined as: The study of fundamental questions about the state, government, politics, liberty, justice, and the enforcement of a legal code by authority.
