Civil war

Political Science

Civil war is a state of sustained, organized armed conflict between a government and at least one internal faction seeking to seize power or secede from the sovereign territory.

Category
Organized conflict
Identity
Armed combat between organized groups within a single sovereign state
Mechanism
the pursuit of political control or territorial secession through violence
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish civil war from riots, coups, or criminal violence by failing to specify the requirement for sustained, organized military-style engagement.

Usually defined as: A war between citizens of the same country.
