Dynamics

Engineering Mechanics

Dynamics is the branch of mechanics that analyzes the motion of physical bodies by calculating how applied forces produce changes in their state of motion.

Category
Branch of mechanics
Identity
Analysis of motion and the forces that produce or change it
Mechanism
Relating the acceleration of a body to the net forces acting upon it through Newton's laws of motion
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish dynamics from kinematics, which describes motion without reference to the forces that cause it.

Usually defined as: The branch of mechanics concerned with the motion of bodies under the action of forces.
