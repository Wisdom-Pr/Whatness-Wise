Feminism

Political Philosophy / Sociology

Feminism is a social and political movement that seeks to achieve gender parity by identifying and dismantling institutionalized structures that historically privilege one gender over others.

Category
Social and political movement
Identity
A collective pursuit of social, political, and economic parity between genders.
Mechanism
The systematic identification and dismantling of institutionalized patriarchal structures that privilege one gender over others.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to identify the mechanism (dismantling structures) and conflates the goal (equality) with the movement itself, which could describe any egalitarian philosophy.

Usually defined as: The advocacy of women's rights on the basis of the equality of the sexes.
