Religious syncretism

Religious Studies

Religious syncretism is the process of merging distinct religious doctrines, rituals, or beliefs into a new, unified system that reconciles previously separate traditions.

Category
Cultural process
Identity
The fusion of disparate religious doctrines, rituals, or practices into a unified or coherent framework.
Mechanism
Selective integration of elements from distinct belief systems to address cultural, social, or spiritual needs.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish syncretism from simple religious pluralism or coexistence, which do not necessarily involve the synthesis of new, unified practices.

Usually defined as: The blending of two or more religious belief systems into a new system, or the incorporation of beliefs from unrelated traditions into a religious tradition.
