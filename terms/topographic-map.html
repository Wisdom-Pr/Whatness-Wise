Topographic map

Geospatial Science

A topographic map is a two-dimensional representation of a three-dimensional landscape that depicts terrain elevation and surface features through the use of contour lines mapped to a specific vertical reference datum.

Category
Cartographic representation
Identity
A two-dimensional projection of a three-dimensional landscape
Mechanism
representing elevation through isolines of constant vertical distance above a reference datum
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify the reference datum and the method of representing the third dimension, potentially confusing it with thematic or relief maps.

Usually defined as: A map that shows the physical features of an area, such as mountains and valleys, using contour lines.
