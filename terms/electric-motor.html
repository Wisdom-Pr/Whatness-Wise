Electric motor

Electrical Engineering

An electric motor is an electromechanical device that converts electrical energy into rotational mechanical energy through the interaction of magnetic fields and current-carrying conductors.

Category
Electromechanical device
Identity
A transducer that converts electrical energy into mechanical motion.
Mechanism
The interaction between magnetic fields and current-carrying conductors to generate torque.
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it fails to distinguish motors from linear actuators or solenoids, and ignores the rotational nature of the device.

Usually defined as: An electric motor is a machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
