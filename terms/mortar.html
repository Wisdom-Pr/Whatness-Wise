Mortar

Construction and Materials

Mortar is a workable paste composed of a binder, fine aggregate, and water that bonds masonry units into a unified structure through the chemical process of hydration.

Category
Construction material
Identity
A workable paste used to bond masonry units into a cohesive structure.
Mechanism
Chemical hydration that transforms a plastic mixture into a rigid, load-bearing solid.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish mortar from concrete, which also uses sand, water, and cement but functions as a structural element rather than a bonding agent.

Usually defined as: A mixture of sand, water, and cement used to bind bricks or stones together.
