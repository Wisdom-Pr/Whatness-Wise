Intertextuality

Literary Studies

Intertextuality is the relational property of a text that derives its meaning and significance through the explicit or implicit presence of other texts within its structure.

Category
Relational property
Identity
The interconnected nature of textual meaning
Mechanism
The shaping of a text's significance through references, allusions, or structural echoes of other works
Resolved
Common definitions are too vague, often failing to distinguish intertextuality from simple plagiarism or direct quotation.

Usually defined as: The relationship between texts, especially literary ones.
