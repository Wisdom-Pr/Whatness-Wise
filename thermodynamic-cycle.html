Thermodynamic cycle

Mechanical Engineering

A thermodynamic cycle is a series of processes that returns a working substance to its initial state, converting heat into work in engines or using work to move heat in refrigerators.

Category
A series of processes
Identity
Returns a working substance to its initial state
Mechanism
Converts heat into work, or uses work to move heat, as in engines and refrigerators
Resolved
Return to initial state is the defining feature
