Carbon footprint

Environmental Science

Carbon footprint is a quantitative measure of the total greenhouse gas emissions, expressed as carbon dioxide equivalent, produced by an entity or product throughout its entire lifecycle.

Category
Environmental impact metric
Identity
A quantitative measure of the total greenhouse gas emissions caused by an entity, event, or product.
Mechanism
Calculated by summing the carbon dioxide equivalent of all relevant greenhouse gases released throughout a defined lifecycle.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify that it is a normalized measurement of multiple gases (not just carbon) and often ignores the lifecycle boundary condition.

Usually defined as: The total amount of greenhouse gases generated by our actions.
