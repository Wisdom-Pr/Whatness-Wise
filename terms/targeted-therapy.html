Targeted therapy

Pharmacology

Targeted therapy is a medical treatment that utilizes drugs to inhibit the growth and spread of cancer by interfering with specific molecular targets involved in tumor progression, rather than by broadly damaging all rapidly dividing cells.

Category
Medical treatment
Identity
Systemic therapeutic intervention
Mechanism
Molecular binding to specific cellular proteins or genetic mutations
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish targeted therapy from traditional chemotherapy, which also targets rapidly dividing cells, and fails to specify the molecular precision that defines this class of drugs.

Usually defined as: Targeted therapy is a type of cancer treatment that uses drugs to identify and attack specific types of cancer cells.
