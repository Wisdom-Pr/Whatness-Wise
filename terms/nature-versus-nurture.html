Nature versus nurture

Psychology
Also: nature vs nurture

Nature versus nurture is a conceptual framework used to analyze how human traits and behaviors emerge through the continuous interaction between inherited biological predispositions and environmental experiences.

Category
Conceptual framework
Identity
A binary model of developmental influence
Mechanism
The interaction between inherited biological predispositions and environmental experiences
Resolved
The standard definition implies a false dichotomy, ignoring the fact that these factors are deeply intertwined and inseparable in development.

Usually defined as: The debate over whether human behavior is determined by genetics or the environment.
