Elevation

Geospatial Science

Elevation is the vertical distance of a point on the Earth's surface measured upward from a standardized global reference level, such as mean sea level.

Category
Geographic coordinate
Identity
Vertical distance of a point relative to a reference geoid or mean sea level.
Mechanism
Measurement of the perpendicular distance from a standardized global reference surface to a specific terrestrial location.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify the reference surface (mean sea level vs. geoid), which is necessary to distinguish elevation from altitude or depth.

Usually defined as: The height of a point above sea level.
