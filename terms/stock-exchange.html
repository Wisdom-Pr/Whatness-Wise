Stock exchange

Finance

A stock exchange is a centralized, regulated marketplace that facilitates the continuous trading of company shares and financial instruments by matching buy and sell orders to establish transparent market prices.

Category
Organized marketplace
Identity
A centralized facility for the continuous exchange of equity shares and financial instruments.
Mechanism
Matching buy and sell orders through a regulated clearing system to establish market-clearing prices.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish an exchange from an over-the-counter market or a private trade, as it omits the requirement for a centralized, regulated, and continuous auction mechanism.

Usually defined as: A market where securities are bought and sold.
