Data science

Computer Science

Data science is a multidisciplinary field that extracts actionable knowledge from complex datasets through the integration of statistical inference, computational algorithms, and domain-specific analysis.

Category
Interdisciplinary field
Identity
A systematic approach to extracting knowledge and actionable insights from structured and unstructured information.
Mechanism
The integration of statistical analysis, computational algorithms, and domain-specific expertise to identify patterns and predictive models.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular and overly narrow, focusing only on 'business' rather than scientific discovery, and fails to distinguish the field from simple data analysis or business intelligence.

Usually defined as: Data science is the study of data to extract meaningful insights for business.
