Assessment

Education

Assessment is the systematic collection and analysis of evidence regarding a subject's performance or state that is compared against established criteria to determine value, progress, or proficiency.

Category
Evaluative process
Identity
The systematic gathering and analysis of evidence regarding a subject's performance or state.
Mechanism
Comparing observed data against established criteria or benchmarks to determine value or progress.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'evaluating' (a synonym for assessment) and fails to distinguish assessment from 'measurement' or 'testing' by omitting the requirement of criteria-based judgment.

Usually defined as: The process of evaluating or estimating the nature, quality, or ability of someone or something.
