Socialization

Sociology

Socialization is the lifelong social process through which individuals internalize the values, beliefs, and behaviors of a society to develop a self-identity and function within that culture.

Category
lifelong social process
Identity
internalizing cultural norms, values, and social roles
Mechanism
interacting with social institutions and cultural agents
Resolved
Standard definitions often frame it as a passive childhood absorption of rules rather than an active, lifelong process of identity formation across changing social environments.

Usually defined as: The process by which individuals learn and internalize the values, norms, and roles of their society.
