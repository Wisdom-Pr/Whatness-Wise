Career development

Human Resource Management

Career development is the lifelong process of managing professional growth through the continuous acquisition of skills, self-assessment, and the strategic navigation of work-related opportunities.

Category
Professional process
Identity
The lifelong sequence of activities and experiences that shape an individual's professional trajectory.
Mechanism
The integration of personal self-assessment, skill acquisition, and strategic navigation of employment opportunities.
Resolved
The standard definition is too vague and circular, often failing to distinguish between 'career' (the path) and 'development' (the growth), and failing to specify that it is a strategic, ongoing process rather than a static state.

Usually defined as: The process of managing your life, learning, and work.
