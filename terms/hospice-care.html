Hospice care

Health Care

Hospice care is a specialized healthcare service for individuals with a terminal prognosis that provides comfort-focused treatment and support to manage symptoms and improve quality of life when curative interventions are no longer pursued.

Category
Healthcare service
Identity
Specialized care for individuals with a terminal prognosis
Mechanism
Providing comfort-focused treatment rather than curative intervention to manage symptoms and improve quality of life
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates hospice with palliative care, failing to distinguish that hospice is specifically restricted to patients with a terminal diagnosis and a limited life expectancy.

Usually defined as: Hospice care is a type of health care that focuses on the palliation of a terminally ill patient's pain and symptoms and attending to their emotional and spiritual needs at the end of life.
