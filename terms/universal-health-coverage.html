Universal health coverage

Health Care
Also: UHC, universal healthcare

Universal health coverage is a healthcare system that ensures all individuals receive necessary medical services without incurring financial ruin by utilizing collective funding mechanisms to remove direct costs at the point of care.

Category
Public policy framework
Identity
A healthcare delivery model ensuring equitable access to essential services
Mechanism
pooling financial resources to eliminate direct out-of-pocket payments at the point of service
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between the goal (access) and the mechanism (financial protection), and it is often confused with 'free' healthcare, which ignores the underlying insurance or tax-based funding structure.

Usually defined as: A system where all people have access to the health services they need without suffering financial hardship.
