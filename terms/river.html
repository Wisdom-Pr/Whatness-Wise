River

Environmental Science

A river is a natural, channelized flow of water and sediment that moves under the influence of gravity from a higher elevation to a lower base level, such as an ocean, lake, or larger water body.

Category
Natural surface water body
Identity
A channelized flow of water driven by gravity
Mechanism
The collection of surface runoff and groundwater within a defined drainage basin to move sediment and water toward a lower base level.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a river from a stream or creek, which are also channelized flows; it also fails to account for the geomorphic role of sediment transport.

Usually defined as: A large natural stream of water flowing in a channel to the sea, a lake, or another such stream.
