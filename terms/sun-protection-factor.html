Sun protection factor

Preventive Medicine
Also: SPF, sunscreen

Sun protection factor is a ratio quantifying the relative increase in the amount of ultraviolet B radiation required to cause skin reddening when a topical product is applied compared to unprotected skin.

Category
Ratio
Identity
A quantitative index of ultraviolet radiation protection.
Mechanism
The ratio of the minimal erythema dose on protected skin to the minimal erythema dose on unprotected skin.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to clarify that it specifically measures protection against UVB radiation rather than all solar radiation, and it risks circularity by using the word 'sun' to explain 'sun protection'.

Usually defined as: A measure of how much solar energy is required to produce sunburn on protected skin relative to unprotected skin.
