Obedience

Psychology

Obedience is a form of social influence where an individual performs an action in response to a direct command issued by a person perceived to hold legitimate authority.

Category
Social behavior
Identity
A form of social influence
Mechanism
The performance of an action in response to a direct command from a perceived authority figure
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish obedience from 'compliance' (which does not require perceived authority) or 'conformity' (which is peer-driven rather than authority-driven).

Usually defined as: Compliance with an order, request, or law or submission to another's authority.
