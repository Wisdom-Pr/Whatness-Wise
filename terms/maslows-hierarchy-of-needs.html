Maslow's hierarchy of needs

Psychology
Also: hierarchy of needs

Maslow's hierarchy of needs is a motivational framework that categorizes human requirements into a tiered structure, where the fulfillment of foundational survival and safety needs serves as the necessary prerequisite for pursuing higher-level psychological and self-actualization goals.

Category
Motivational theory
Identity
A hierarchical model of human requirements
Mechanism
Prioritization of physiological and safety foundations to enable the pursuit of growth-oriented psychological objectives
Resolved
The standard definition often implies a rigid, linear progression that ignores individual variation and cultural context, failing to distinguish the theory from a prescriptive rule.

Usually defined as: A theory of human motivation proposing that people must satisfy basic needs before progressing to higher-level psychological and self-fulfillment needs.
