Universal basic income

Social Policy
Also: UBI

Universal basic income is a periodic cash payment delivered to all members of a population on an individual basis without a means test or work requirement.

Category
Economic policy
Identity
Periodic cash transfer
Mechanism
Unconditional distribution to all members of a population regardless of their income level or employment status
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish UBI from conditional welfare or negative income tax by omitting the requirement of universality and unconditionality.

Usually defined as: A government program in which every adult citizen receives a set amount of money on a regular basis.
