Force

Physics / Structural Engineering

Force is a push or pull one object exerts on another as a whole, changing the object's state of motion or structural form.

CategoryA push or pull
IdentityExerted by one object on another, as a whole
MechanismChanges the object's state of motion or structural form
ResolvedPhysics-only phrasing missed static deformation (engineering); "as a whole" added to separate it from pressure/stress
