Famine

Development Studies

Famine is a widespread, prolonged shortage of food resources within a human population that results in excess mortality due to the failure of food availability or access.

Category
Socio-economic phenomenon
Identity
A widespread, prolonged shortage of food resources within a human population.
Mechanism
The failure of food availability or access relative to the nutritional requirements of a population, resulting in excess mortality.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish famine from localized food insecurity or simple hunger by omitting the critical threshold of excess mortality.

Usually defined as: An extreme scarcity of food in a region.
