Strategic planning

Business

Strategic planning is a systematic organizational process that directs long-term resource allocation by analyzing internal capabilities and external market conditions to define and achieve specific, measurable objectives.

Category
Management process
Identity
A systematic method for aligning organizational resources with long-term objectives.
Mechanism
Analyzing internal capabilities and external environmental factors to prioritize resource allocation and define measurable goals.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'planning' to define 'strategic planning' and fails to distinguish the concept from general goal-setting or operational budgeting.

Usually defined as: Strategic planning is the process of documenting and establishing a direction for an organization by assessing where you are and where you're going.
