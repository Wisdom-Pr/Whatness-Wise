Deep sleep

Preventive Medicine

Deep sleep is the stage of non-rapid eye movement sleep characterized by synchronized, high-amplitude delta brain waves that facilitate essential metabolic waste clearance and physical tissue repair.

Category
Physiological state
Identity
A phase of non-rapid eye movement sleep marked by high-amplitude, low-frequency delta brain waves.
Mechanism
Synchronized neural firing across the cerebral cortex that facilitates metabolic waste clearance and tissue repair.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish deep sleep from other NREM stages and relies on the vague term 'restoration' without identifying the neurological mechanism of delta wave synchronization.

Usually defined as: Deep sleep is the stage of sleep characterized by slow brain waves and physical restoration.
