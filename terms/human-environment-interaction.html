Human–environment interaction

Geography
Also: human-environment interaction

Human–environment interaction is the bidirectional process through which human societies modify natural ecosystems and are simultaneously shaped by the constraints and opportunities of those environments.

Category
Relational process
Identity
The reciprocal exchange of influence between human social systems and natural ecosystems.
Mechanism
The modification of physical landscapes by human activity and the adaptation of human societies to environmental constraints.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular and fails to distinguish the concept from general 'interaction,' lacking the specific focus on the bidirectional feedback loop between society and nature.

Usually defined as: The ways in which humans interact with the environment and how the environment affects humans.
