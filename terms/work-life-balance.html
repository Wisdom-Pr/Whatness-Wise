Work-life balance

Human Resource Management

Work-life balance is the subjective state of satisfaction achieved when an individual effectively allocates their time and energy between professional obligations and personal pursuits to prevent role conflict.

Category
Social construct
Identity
The prioritization of professional and personal demands
Mechanism
The allocation of time and energy across distinct life domains
Resolved
The standard definition is circular (using 'life' to define 'work-life') and fails to distinguish the concept from simple time management by ignoring the subjective perception of satisfaction.

Usually defined as: Work-life balance is the state of equilibrium between an individual's professional responsibilities and their personal life.
