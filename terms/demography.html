Demography

Demography is the statistical study of human populations that analyzes the size, structure, and distribution of groups through the quantitative measurement of birth, death, migration, and aging rates.

Category
Scientific discipline
Identity
Statistical analysis of human population structure and change
Mechanism
Mathematical modeling of birth, death, migration, and aging patterns
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish demography from general sociology or geography by omitting the specific focus on vital statistics and quantitative population dynamics.

Usually defined as: The study of human populations, especially their size, density, and distribution.
