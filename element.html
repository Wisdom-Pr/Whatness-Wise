Element

Chemistry

An element is a pure substance composed entirely of atoms with the same number of protons, and cannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical reactions.

CategoryA pure substance
IdentityComposed entirely of atoms with the same number of protons
MechanismCannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical reactions
Resolved"Making it impossible to... through" tightened to "and cannot be... by" for concision; discriminates correctly against compound (which chemical reactions CAN break down)
