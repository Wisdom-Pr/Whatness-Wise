Carbon sequestration

Environmental Science

Carbon sequestration is the long-term removal of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere into stable geological, biological, or oceanic reservoirs to mitigate climate change.

Category
Geochemical process
Identity
The long-term isolation of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere into geological, biological, or oceanic reservoirs.
Mechanism
The transfer of carbon from the atmosphere into stable sinks through natural biological uptake or engineered chemical capture and injection.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between the act of capture and the requirement of long-term storage, and it ignores the distinction between natural cycles and industrial mitigation.

Usually defined as: The process of capturing and storing atmospheric carbon dioxide.
