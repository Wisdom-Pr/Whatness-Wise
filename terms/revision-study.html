Revision (study)

Study Skills
Also: revision

Revision is a study strategy that re-engages previously learned information through active retrieval, testing, or reorganization to strengthen long-term memory retention.

Category
study strategy
Identity
re-engagement with previously learned information
Mechanism
active retrieval, testing, or reorganization of knowledge
Resolved
Common definitions describe it passively as merely 'reviewing' or 'looking over' notes, failing to distinguish active memory consolidation from passive reading.

Usually defined as: The process of reviewing previously learned material to prepare for an examination.
