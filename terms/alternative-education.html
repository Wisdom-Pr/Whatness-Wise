Alternative education

Education

Alternative education is a pedagogical framework that operates outside the conventional institutional structure by employing non-standardized curricula, assessment methods, or learning environments to accommodate diverse student needs.

Category
Educational model
Identity
A pedagogical framework operating outside the conventional K-12 institutional structure.
Mechanism
The intentional deviation from standardized curricula, assessment methods, or instructional environments to accommodate diverse learner needs.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the term 'traditional' to define 'alternative' without specifying the structural or pedagogical divergence.

Usually defined as: Educational approaches that differ from the traditional public school system.
