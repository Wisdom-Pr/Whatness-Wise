Discourse

Media and Communication

Discourse is a structured system of language and social practice that shapes how knowledge is produced and understood within a specific cultural or institutional context.

Category
Communication process
Identity
A structured system of language and social practice
Mechanism
the exchange of ideas within a specific cultural or institutional context that shapes how knowledge is produced and understood
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, failing to distinguish discourse from simple conversation or generic speech by ignoring its role in constructing social reality and power dynamics.

Usually defined as: A written or spoken communication or debate.
