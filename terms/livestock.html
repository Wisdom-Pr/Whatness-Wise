Livestock

Agriculture

Livestock are domesticated animals raised in an agricultural setting to provide commodities such as food, fiber, or labor through human-managed breeding and care.

Category
Domesticated animals
Identity
Animals raised in an agricultural setting to produce commodities or labor.
Mechanism
Selective breeding and controlled husbandry for human utility.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'farm' (a place where livestock are kept) and fails to distinguish livestock from pets or wildlife kept in captivity.

Usually defined as: Animals kept on a farm for profit or use.
