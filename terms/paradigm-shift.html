Paradigm shift

Research Methods

Paradigm shift is a radical transformation in the foundational concepts and practices of a scientific community that occurs when a new model replaces an existing one to resolve persistent anomalies.

Category
Conceptual framework
Identity
A radical transformation in the foundational concepts, practices, and values shared by a scientific community.
Mechanism
The replacement of an existing model of reality with a new, incompatible model that resolves anomalies the previous framework could not explain.
Resolved
The standard definition is too vague, failing to distinguish a paradigm shift from a simple change in opinion or a minor methodological update.

Usually defined as: A fundamental change in approach or underlying assumptions.
