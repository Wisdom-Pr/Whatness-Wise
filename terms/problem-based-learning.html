Problem-based learning

Education
Also: PBL

Problem-based learning is an instructional method where students acquire knowledge through the collaborative analysis and resolution of a complex, open-ended problem that serves as the initial stimulus for learning.

Category
Pedagogical strategy
Identity
Instructional method
Mechanism
Guided inquiry and collaborative resolution of complex, real-world scenarios
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the method from general project-based learning or simple case studies, as it does not explicitly require the problem to precede the acquisition of knowledge.

Usually defined as: A student-centered approach in which students learn about a subject through the experience of solving an open-ended problem.
