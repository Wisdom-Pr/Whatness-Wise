Pop art

Visual Arts

Pop art is an artistic movement that challenges the boundaries between high culture and commercial mass media by recontextualizing the imagery and aesthetic techniques of consumer products and popular iconography.

Category
Artistic movement
Identity
A visual style incorporating iconography from commercial and consumer culture.
Mechanism
Appropriating and recontextualizing recognizable mass-produced imagery to challenge traditional distinctions between fine art and commercial design.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish Pop Art from other movements like Dada or Surrealism that also used found objects, and it omits the movement's specific focus on the aesthetic of consumerism.

Usually defined as: An art movement that emerged in the 1950s that uses imagery from popular and mass culture.
