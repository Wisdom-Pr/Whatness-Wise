Medical imaging

Medicine

Medical imaging is the non-invasive process of capturing and processing energy signals from internal biological structures to create visual representations for the purpose of clinical diagnosis and treatment planning.

Category
Diagnostic procedure
Identity
Non-invasive visualization of internal biological structures and physiological functions.
Mechanism
The capture and processing of energy signals, such as electromagnetic radiation or sound waves, reflected or emitted by internal tissues.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish medical imaging from general photography or industrial scanning by omitting the clinical purpose and the specific biological interaction required for diagnosis.

Usually defined as: The technique used to create visual representations of the interior of a body for clinical analysis and medical intervention.
