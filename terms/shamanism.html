Shamanism

Religious Studies

Shamanism is a religious practice centered on a mediator who intentionally alters their state of consciousness to interact with the non-physical realm and influence spiritual forces on behalf of a community.

Category
Religious and social practice
Identity
A system of spiritual belief and practice centered on a mediator who interacts with the non-physical realm.
Mechanism
The intentional alteration of conscious states to facilitate communication with or influence over spiritual entities for the benefit of a community.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the term 'shaman' to define 'shamanism' and fails to distinguish it from general spirit-possession or priesthood.

Usually defined as: The belief in a hidden world of spirits that can be influenced by a shaman.
