Respiratory syncytial virus

Public Health
Also: RSV

Respiratory syncytial virus is a single-stranded RNA virus that causes respiratory illness by infecting epithelial cells and inducing the formation of multinucleated giant cells.

Category
Pathogenic virus
Identity
A single-stranded RNA virus of the Pneumoviridae family
Mechanism
infecting the epithelial cells of the respiratory tract and inducing the formation of multinucleated giant cells
Resolved
The standard definition describes the symptoms rather than the biological identity and fails to distinguish it from other common respiratory pathogens like influenza or rhinovirus.

Usually defined as: A common respiratory virus that usually causes mild, cold-like symptoms.
