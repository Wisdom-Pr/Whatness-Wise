Sarcopenia

Medicine

Sarcopenia is the age-related, involuntary loss of skeletal muscle mass and functional strength that impairs physical mobility and metabolic health.

Category
Pathological condition
Identity
Involuntary, progressive reduction of skeletal muscle tissue and functional capacity.
Mechanism
Imbalance between protein synthesis and degradation exacerbated by aging, hormonal decline, and physical inactivity.
Resolved
The standard definition often omits the distinction between simple muscle loss (atrophy) and the functional impairment (strength/power loss) that defines the clinical syndrome.

Usually defined as: The age-related loss of muscle mass and strength.
