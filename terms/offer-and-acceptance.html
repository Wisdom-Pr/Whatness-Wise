Offer and acceptance

Law

Offer and acceptance is the process of mutual assent that creates a legally binding contract when one party proposes specific terms and the other party unequivocally agrees to them.

Category
Legal mechanism
Identity
The foundational process of mutual assent required to form a binding agreement.
Mechanism
The communication of a specific promise followed by an unequivocal manifestation of agreement to the exact terms proposed.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the formation of a contract from mere negotiation or preliminary discussions.

Usually defined as: An offer is a proposal to enter into a contract, and acceptance is the agreement to those terms.
