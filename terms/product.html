Product

Chemistry

Product is a tangible good or intangible service created through labor and capital that is offered to a market to satisfy a consumer need or want.

Category
Economic output
Identity
A tangible good or intangible service offered to a market for acquisition, use, or consumption.
Mechanism
The transformation of raw materials or knowledge through labor and capital into a value-adding entity.
Resolved
The standard definition fails discrimination because it could describe a byproduct, waste, or a personal hobby project; it lacks the requirement of market exchangeability.

Usually defined as: A thing that is produced by labor or effort.
