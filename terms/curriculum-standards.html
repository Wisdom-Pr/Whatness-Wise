Curriculum standards

Education
Also: curriculum standard

Curriculum standards are the mandatory benchmarks of student knowledge and skill attainment that define the required learning outcomes for a specific grade level or subject.

Category
Educational policy instrument
Identity
Defined benchmarks for student knowledge and skills
Mechanism
Mandating specific content mastery and performance expectations across an educational system
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between 'standards' (the goals) and 'curriculum' (the materials/methods) and often ignores the accountability mechanism that differentiates standards from mere suggestions.

Usually defined as: Curriculum standards are the learning goals for what students should know and be able to do at each grade level.
