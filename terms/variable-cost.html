Variable cost

Accounting

Variable cost is an expense that changes in direct proportion to the volume of output produced, remaining constant on a per-unit basis.

Category
Economic expenditure
Identity
A cost that fluctuates in direct correlation with production volume.
Mechanism
The application of a per-unit resource requirement to the total quantity of output generated.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish variable costs from 'step costs' or 'semi-variable costs' which also change with volume but not in direct proportion.

Usually defined as: Costs that change in proportion to the level of goods or services produced.
