Andragogy

Education

Andragogy is a framework for adult learning that facilitates the acquisition of knowledge through the learner's self-directed inquiry and the integration of their existing life experiences into practical, task-oriented problem-solving.

Category
Educational Theory
Identity
A framework for adult learning
Mechanism
Self-directed inquiry and the application of life experience to problem-solving
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish andragogy from general pedagogy, which also involves teaching; it misses the requirement that the learner must be self-directed and the curriculum must be task-oriented rather than subject-oriented.

Usually defined as: The method and practice of teaching adult learners.
