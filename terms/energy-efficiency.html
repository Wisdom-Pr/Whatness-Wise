Energy efficiency

Energy Engineering

Energy efficiency is the ratio of useful work or service produced to the total energy consumed by a system, representing the extent to which energy losses are minimized.

Category
Performance metric
Identity
The ratio of useful output to total energy input in a system.
Mechanism
Minimizing energy losses through technological or operational optimization.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular because it uses the word 'energy' to define 'energy efficiency' and fails to specify the ratio relationship between input and output.

Usually defined as: The goal of using less energy to provide the same amount of energy service.
