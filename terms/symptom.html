Symptom

Medicine

Symptom is a subjective experience of change in bodily or mental function that a patient reports as an indicator of an underlying health condition.

Category
Clinical indicator
Identity
A subjective experience of change in bodily or mental function reported by a patient.
Mechanism
Manifestation of an underlying physiological or psychological pathology.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates symptoms with signs; signs are objective findings observed by a clinician, whereas symptoms are exclusively subjective experiences reported by the sufferer.

Usually defined as: A physical or mental feature which is regarded as indicating a condition of disease, particularly such a feature that is apparent to the patient.
