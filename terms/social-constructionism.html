Social constructionism

Sociology

Social constructionism is a theory of knowledge asserting that the categories and concepts humans use to perceive reality are not inherent to the natural world, but are produced through ongoing social interaction and shared language.

Category
Sociological theory
Identity
A framework for understanding human knowledge and reality
Mechanism
The process of shared social interaction and linguistic exchange
Resolved
The standard definition is overly broad and fails to distinguish the theory from general socialization or developmental psychology by omitting the core claim that 'objective' reality is itself a product of these interactions.

Usually defined as: A theory that holds that human development is socially situated and knowledge is constructed through interaction with others.
