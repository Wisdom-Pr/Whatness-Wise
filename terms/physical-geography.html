Physical geography

Geography

Physical geography is the study of the Earth's natural features and processes that analyzes how the interactions between the atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, and biosphere create distinct spatial patterns across the surface of the planet.

Category
Academic discipline
Identity
A field of study focused on the spatial patterns and processes of the Earth's natural environment.
Mechanism
The systematic analysis of the interactions between the lithosphere, atmosphere, hydrosphere, and biosphere.
Resolved
The standard definition is overly broad and fails to distinguish the field from general environmental science or geology by omitting the spatial perspective.

Usually defined as: The branch of geography concerned with natural features and phenomena of the earth's surface.
