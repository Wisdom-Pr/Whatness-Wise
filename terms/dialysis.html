Dialysis

Pharmacology

Dialysis is a separation process that removes specific solutes from a fluid by allowing them to pass through a semipermeable membrane based on their molecular size relative to the membrane's pores.

Category
Physiological process
Identity
Selective separation of solutes based on molecular size
Mechanism
Diffusion across a semipermeable membrane driven by a concentration gradient
Resolved
The standard definition focuses only on clinical kidney treatment, ignoring the fundamental biochemical mechanism of solute separation used in laboratory research and chemical engineering.

Usually defined as: The process of removing waste products and excess fluid from the blood when the kidneys are not working properly.
