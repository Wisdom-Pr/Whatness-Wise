Epidemiological transition

Epidemiology

Epidemiological transition is a shift in the pattern of population health where the primary causes of death change from infectious and communicable diseases to chronic and non-communicable conditions as a society undergoes socioeconomic development.

Category
Demographic model
Identity
A long-term shift in the primary causes of mortality and morbidity within a population.
Mechanism
The transition from infectious and parasitic diseases to chronic, degenerative, and lifestyle-related conditions.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the concept with the 'Demographic Transition' (which focuses on birth/death rates) and fails to specify the unique shift in disease profiles that defines this specific term.

Usually defined as: The shift from high birth and death rates to low birth and death rates as a country develops.
