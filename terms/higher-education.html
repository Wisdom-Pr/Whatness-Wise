Higher education

Education

Higher education is the system of post-secondary instruction and research that grants formal academic degrees or professional credentials through structured, advanced curricula.

Category
Tertiary educational system
Identity
Structured academic and professional instruction leading to formal degrees or certifications.
Mechanism
Institutionalized curriculum delivery and assessment of advanced knowledge and specialized skills.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'education' to define 'higher education' and fails to distinguish it from vocational training or informal learning.

Usually defined as: Education provided by universities and colleges after high school.
