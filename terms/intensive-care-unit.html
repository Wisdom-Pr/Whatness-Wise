Intensive care unit

Health Care
Also: ICU

An intensive care unit is a specialized hospital department that provides continuous physiological monitoring and advanced life-support interventions for patients experiencing acute, life-threatening organ failure.

Category
Healthcare facility
Identity
A specialized hospital department designed for the continuous monitoring and support of patients with critical physiological instability.
Mechanism
The integration of advanced life-support technology and constant professional surveillance to stabilize and restore vital organ function.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the term 'intensive care' to define the unit, and it fails to distinguish the unit from other specialized wards like surgery or recovery by omitting the requirement for continuous physiological monitoring.

Usually defined as: A specialized hospital ward that provides intensive care for patients with severe or life-threatening illnesses and injuries.
