Shareholder

Business

A shareholder is an individual or entity that holds legal ownership of a corporation's equity, granting them the right to vote on corporate governance and a proportional claim on the company's residual assets and earnings.

Category
Legal and financial entity
Identity
An individual or entity holding legal ownership of a portion of a corporation's equity.
Mechanism
The possession of equity shares, which confers specific ownership rights including voting on corporate governance and receiving a proportional claim on residual assets and earnings.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish shareholders from other stakeholders (like employees or creditors) who also have interests in the firm but lack ownership rights.

Usually defined as: A person or institution that owns at least one share of stock in a public or private corporation.
