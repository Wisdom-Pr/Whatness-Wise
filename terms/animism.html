Animism

Religious Studies

Animism is a worldview that attributes agency and spiritual essence to non-human entities, including animals, plants, and inanimate objects, to explain their roles and interactions within the natural environment.

Category
System of belief
Identity
Attribution of consciousness or soul to non-human entities
Mechanism
Projecting intentionality onto natural phenomena to explain their behavior or existence
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish animism from pantheism or simple personification, and often implies a primitive 'belief' rather than a cognitive framework for interacting with the environment.

Usually defined as: The belief that objects, places, and creatures all possess a distinct spiritual essence.
