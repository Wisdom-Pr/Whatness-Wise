Supply and demand

Economics

Supply and demand is an economic model that determines the market price and quantity of a good or service through the interaction of the quantity producers are willing to sell and the quantity consumers are willing to purchase at varying price levels.

Category
Economic model
Identity
A framework for determining market equilibrium price and quantity.
Mechanism
The interaction of independent buyer and seller behaviors in a competitive market.
Resolved
Common definitions often fail to specify that the relationship is a functional dependency on price, and they often treat supply and demand as two separate concepts rather than a single integrated mechanism for price discovery.

Usually defined as: The relationship between the quantity of a commodity that producers wish to sell at various prices and the quantity that consumers wish to buy.
