Social-emotional learning

Education
Also: SEL

Social-emotional learning is the systematic process of developing the cognitive and affective skills required to regulate emotions, navigate social environments, and make ethical decisions through structured educational practice.

Category
Educational framework
Identity
A developmental process of acquiring interpersonal and intrapersonal competencies.
Mechanism
Integrating cognitive, affective, and behavioral strategies within academic and social environments.
Resolved
The standard definition is a list of outcomes rather than a definition of the framework itself, and it fails to distinguish the process from general socialization.

Usually defined as: The process through which children and adults acquire and effectively apply the knowledge, attitudes, and skills necessary to understand and manage emotions, set and achieve positive goals, feel and show empathy for others, establish and maintain positive relationships, and make responsible decisions.
