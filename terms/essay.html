Essay

Research Methods

An essay is a focused piece of analytical or argumentative prose that explores a specific subject through the systematic development of a central thesis supported by evidence and logical reasoning.

Category
Literary composition
Identity
A focused analytical or argumentative prose work
Mechanism
the systematic development of a central thesis through evidence and logical reasoning
Resolved
The standard definition is too broad, as it could describe a report, a news article, or a textbook chapter, and fails to distinguish the essay's requirement for a personal or critical perspective.

Usually defined as: A short piece of writing on a particular subject.
