Echo chamber

Media and Communication

Echo chamber is a closed communication environment that reinforces a user's existing beliefs by systematically excluding or delegitimizing dissenting perspectives.

Category
Social environment
Identity
A closed communication system
Mechanism
The systematic exclusion of dissenting perspectives and the amplification of pre-existing beliefs through selective exposure.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish an echo chamber from a 'filter bubble' (which is algorithmically driven) or a 'homogenous community' (which is socially driven); it also fails to specify that the mechanism requires the active reinforcement of bias.

Usually defined as: An environment where a person only encounters information or opinions that reflect and reinforce their own.
