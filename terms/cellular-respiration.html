Cellular respiration

Biology

Cellular respiration is a metabolic process that extracts chemical energy from organic molecules by oxidizing them to produce adenosine triphosphate as the primary energy currency for cellular work.

Category
Metabolic process
Identity
A series of biochemical reactions that extract chemical energy from organic molecules.
Mechanism
The oxidation of glucose coupled with the reduction of electron carriers to generate adenosine triphosphate (ATP).
Resolved
The standard definition fails Rule 7 by omitting the specific energy carrier (ATP) and Rule 5 by failing to distinguish respiration from fermentation or simple combustion.

Usually defined as: The process by which cells convert nutrients into energy.
