Voice (grammar)

English Language
Also: voice

Voice is the grammatical category that defines the relationship between the subject and the action of a verb by determining whether the subject functions as the agent, the patient, or the recipient of the verb's semantic content.

Category
Grammatical category
Identity
The relationship between the subject of a clause and the action expressed by the verb.
Mechanism
The mapping of semantic roles to syntactic positions, specifically whether the subject is the agent or the patient of the action.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to account for middle voice or ergative constructions where the subject's role is not simply 'performing' or 'receiving' an action.

Usually defined as: Voice is the form of a verb that shows whether the subject of the sentence performs or receives the action.
