Subject (grammar)

English Language
Also: subject

A subject is the noun phrase that maintains grammatical agreement with the finite verb to identify the primary entity or state performing the action or existing in a clause.

Category
Grammatical constituent
Identity
The noun phrase that functions as the primary participant of a clause's predicate.
Mechanism
Agreement with the finite verb in person and number within a sentence structure.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using 'doing or being' (verbs) to define the subject, and it fails to distinguish the subject from other noun phrases like objects or complements.

Usually defined as: The person, place, thing, or idea that is doing or being something in a sentence.
