Property law

Law

Property law is the branch of jurisprudence that establishes the legal relationship between individuals and their assets by defining exclusive rights that are enforceable against the entire world.

Category
Legal system
Identity
The framework of rules defining rights and obligations regarding the control and transfer of tangible and intangible assets.
Mechanism
The formal recognition and enforcement of exclusive legal claims by the state against the interference of others.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish property law from contract law, as both involve rights and obligations; it also fails to specify that property rights are 'in rem' (against the world) rather than 'in personam' (against specific individuals).

Usually defined as: The branch of law that governs the ownership and use of land and personal possessions.
