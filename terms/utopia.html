Utopia

Literature

Utopia is a theoretical model of a society that achieves perfect sociopolitical, legal, and moral harmony through the total elimination of structural conflict and material scarcity.

Category
Social construct
Identity
A theoretical model of a society possessing perfect sociopolitical, legal, and moral systems.
Mechanism
The systematic elimination of conflict, scarcity, and inequality through idealized institutional design.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish utopia from heaven or fantasy, ignoring that it is specifically a model for human governance rather than a supernatural state.

Usually defined as: An imagined place or state of things in which everything is perfect.
