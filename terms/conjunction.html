Conjunction

English Language

Conjunction is a functional word or operator that links two or more words, phrases, clauses, or logical propositions to establish a specific relationship of coordination or subordination between them.

Category
Functional linguistic unit
Identity
A linking element that establishes a relationship between two or more grammatical components or logical propositions.
Mechanism
Syntactic coordination or subordination that joins elements of equal or unequal rank to form a unified structure or compound proposition.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish conjunctions from prepositions, which also link words, and fails to account for the logical usage of conjunctions in formal reasoning.

Usually defined as: A word used to connect clauses or sentences or to coordinate words in the same clause.
