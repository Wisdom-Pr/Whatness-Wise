Decision theory

Management

Decision theory is the mathematical framework for selecting an optimal course of action among multiple alternatives by calculating the expected utility of outcomes under conditions of uncertainty.

Category
Interdisciplinary analytical framework
Identity
A formal study of strategies for selecting an optimal course of action among multiple alternatives under conditions of uncertainty or risk.
Mechanism
The application of mathematical models and probability to evaluate the expected utility of potential outcomes.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by using the word 'decisions' and fails to distinguish the theory from simple choice-making by omitting the requirement for mathematical optimization under uncertainty.

Usually defined as: The study of how people make choices and decisions.
