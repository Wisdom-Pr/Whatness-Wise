Institutional review board

Education
Also: IRB, ethics committee

An institutional review board is a committee that protects the rights and welfare of human research participants by mandating that all proposed studies meet established ethical and regulatory standards before and during their execution.

Category
Administrative committee
Identity
A regulatory body tasked with the oversight of research involving human participants.
Mechanism
Applying ethical standards and federal regulations to evaluate the risk-to-benefit ratio of proposed study protocols.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the board's function from general ethics committees by omitting its specific legal mandate to approve, monitor, and require modifications to research protocols before they commence.

Usually defined as: An institutional review board (IRB) is a committee that reviews research involving human subjects to ensure it is ethical and complies with regulations.
