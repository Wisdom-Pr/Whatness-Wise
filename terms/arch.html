Arch

Structural Engineering

An arch is a curved structural member designed to span an opening by converting vertical gravitational loads into compressive forces that are directed along the curve to the supports.

Category
Structural element
Identity
A curved structural member designed to span an opening.
Mechanism
Converting vertical gravitational loads into compressive forces that are directed along the curve to the supports.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish an arch from a beam or lintel, which also span openings but resist loads primarily through bending rather than compression.

Usually defined as: A curved structure that spans an opening and supports weight from above.
