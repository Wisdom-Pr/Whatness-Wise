Cognitive psychology

Psychology

Cognitive psychology is the scientific study of internal mental processes that transform, store, and retrieve information, modeled through the analysis of observable behavior and neural activity.

Category
Scientific field of study
Identity
The systematic investigation of internal mental states and the processes that transform sensory input into knowledge and behavior.
Mechanism
The application of the scientific method to model and measure unobservable mental operations, such as attention, language processing, and problem-solving, through observable behavioral and neural data.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular because it defines a field by listing its own sub-processes without identifying the underlying scientific methodology that distinguishes it from general psychology.

Usually defined as: The branch of psychology concerned with mental processes such as perception, thinking, learning, and memory.
