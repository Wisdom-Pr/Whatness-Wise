Pathophysiology

Medicine

Pathophysiology is the study of the functional changes in cells, tissues, and organs that result from disease or injury, distinguishing the progression of physiological breakdown from the initial cause of the condition.

Category
Scientific discipline
Identity
The study of disordered physiological processes associated with disease or injury.
Mechanism
Analyzing the functional changes in cells, tissues, and organs that occur during the progression of a pathological state.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular because it uses 'disease' to define a field that studies the mechanisms of disease, and it fails to distinguish between the study of the cause (etiology) and the study of the resulting functional breakdown.

Usually defined as: The study of how disease processes affect the normal function of the body.
