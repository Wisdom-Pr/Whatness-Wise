Social structure

Sociology

Social structure is the enduring pattern of roles, institutions, and relationships that organizes a group by defining expected behaviors and regulating access to resources.

Category
enduring framework
Identity
pattern of roles, institutions, and relationships
Mechanism
organizing a group by defining expected behaviors and regulating access to resources
Resolved
Relies on circular phrasing like 'social relationships in society' without distinguishing the underlying framework from culture or temporary group interactions.

Usually defined as: The organized pattern of social relationships and social institutions that together compose society.
