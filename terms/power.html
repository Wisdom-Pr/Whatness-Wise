Power

Physics

Power is the scalar quantity representing the rate at which energy is transferred or converted, calculated as the amount of energy exchanged divided by the time taken for that exchange.

Category
Scalar quantity
Identity
The temporal rate of energy conversion or transfer within a system.
Mechanism
The quotient of energy exchanged divided by the time interval of the exchange.
Resolved
The standard definition often omits the temporal component or confuses the quantity with the capacity to perform work (energy).

Usually defined as: The rate at which work is done or energy is transferred.
