Domestic violence

Social Welfare
Also: intimate partner violence

Domestic violence is a pattern of abusive behavior within an intimate or domestic relationship that inflicts physical, psychological, or economic harm to establish power and control over another person.

Category
Interpersonal behavior
Identity
Systemic pattern of coercive control
Mechanism
Physical, emotional, sexual, or economic abuse within a domestic or intimate relationship
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the behavior (abuse) with the intent (control), potentially excluding cases where the abuse is reactive or lacks a clear power-seeking motive, and fails to distinguish it from general assault.

Usually defined as: A pattern of abusive behavior in any relationship that is used by one partner to gain or maintain power and control over another intimate partner.
