Atomic mass

Chemistry

Atomic mass is the total mass of a single atom, calculated from the sum of its protons, neutrons, and electrons relative to one-twelfth the mass of a carbon-12 atom.

Category
Physical quantity
Identity
Total mass of a single, individual atom
Mechanism
Summing the masses of its protons, neutrons, and electrons relative to a carbon-12 standard
Resolved
Standard definitions frequently confuse atomic mass with atomic weight (a sample's weighted average across isotopes) or mass number (an integer count of nucleons).

Usually defined as: The mass of an atom of a chemical element expressed in atomic mass units.
