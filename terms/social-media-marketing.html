Social media marketing

Marketing

Social media marketing is the strategic use of digital network platforms to influence consumer behavior through the systematic creation and targeted distribution of content designed to elicit measurable audience engagement.

Category
Marketing strategy
Identity
A process of leveraging digital network platforms to influence consumer perception and behavior.
Mechanism
The systematic creation and distribution of content designed to elicit engagement and data-driven interaction within online communities.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular (uses 'social media' to define 'social media marketing') and fails to distinguish the practice from general social media usage by individuals.

Usually defined as: The use of social media platforms to connect with your audience to build your brand, increase sales, and drive website traffic.
