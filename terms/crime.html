Crime

Law

A crime is an act or omission that the law forbids and that the state can prosecute and punish as an offence against society.

Category
An act or omission
Identity
Prohibited by law and punishable by the state
Mechanism
Prosecuted as an offence against society
Resolved
State punishment is the discriminator from a civil wrong
