Fluid mechanics

Physics

Fluid mechanics is the branch of physics that studies the behavior of liquids and gases by applying the laws of conservation to substances that deform continuously when subjected to shear stress.

Category
Scientific discipline
Identity
The study of the physical behavior of substances that deform continuously under applied shear stress.
Mechanism
Applying the principles of conservation of mass, momentum, and energy to substances that lack a fixed shape.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular, as it uses the word 'mechanics' to define 'fluid mechanics', and fails to specify the unique physical property (continuous deformation under shear) that distinguishes fluids from solids.

Usually defined as: The branch of science concerned with the mechanics of fluids (liquids and gases) and the forces on them.
