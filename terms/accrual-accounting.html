Accrual accounting

Accounting

Accrual accounting is the accounting method that records revenue when it is earned and expenses when they are incurred, regardless of when cash actually changes hands.

Category
An accounting method
Identity
Records revenue and expenses when earned or incurred
Mechanism
Independent of when cash is received or paid
Resolved
Timing rule is the discriminator from cash basis
