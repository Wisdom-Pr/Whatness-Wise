Placebo effect

Medical Research

Placebo effect is a measurable improvement in a patient's health condition produced by the patient's expectation of recovery and the therapeutic context rather than the pharmacological or physical properties of the intervention.

Category
Psychological and physiological response
Identity
A measurable improvement in a patient's condition following the administration of an inert substance or sham procedure.
Mechanism
The patient's expectation of recovery and the therapeutic context triggering neurobiological changes such as the release of endogenous opioids or dopamine.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the effect from the natural history of a disease or regression to the mean, which are distinct phenomena that also cause perceived improvement.

Usually defined as: The phenomenon where a patient feels better after receiving a treatment that has no active therapeutic effect.
