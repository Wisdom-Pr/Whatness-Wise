Clause (grammar)

English Language
Also: clause

A clause is a syntactic unit consisting of a subject and a finite verb phrase that functions as a constituent within a sentence to express a proposition.

Category
Syntactic unit
Identity
A grammatical structure consisting of a subject and a finite verb phrase
Mechanism
functioning as a constituent within a sentence to express a complete or partial proposition
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish a clause from a simple sentence, as many clauses are dependent and cannot stand alone as a sentence.

Usually defined as: A group of words containing a subject and a predicate.
