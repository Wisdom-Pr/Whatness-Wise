Blended learning

Education

Blended learning is an instructional model that combines face-to-face classroom interaction with digital online content to provide students with increased control over the time, place, path, or pace of their learning.

Category
Instructional model
Identity
A hybrid pedagogical approach combining face-to-face instruction with digital, student-paced online components.
Mechanism
The integration of physical classroom interaction with digital content delivery to allow for student control over time, place, path, or pace.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish blended learning from simple 'technology-enhanced' learning, which does not necessarily shift control of the learning path to the student.

Usually defined as: A method of teaching that integrates online digital media with traditional classroom methods.
