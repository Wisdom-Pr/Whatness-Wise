Active learning

Education

Active learning is an instructional strategy that requires students to engage with course material through analysis, synthesis, or evaluation, shifting the primary cognitive load from the instructor's delivery to the student's own mental processing.

Category
Instructional strategy
Identity
A pedagogical framework requiring student engagement with course material through analysis, synthesis, or evaluation.
Mechanism
Shifting the primary cognitive load from the instructor's delivery to the student's active processing of information.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular, using the word 'learning' to define 'active learning', and fails to distinguish it from simple classroom participation or group work.

Usually defined as: An instructional approach where students participate in the learning process rather than passively taking in information.
