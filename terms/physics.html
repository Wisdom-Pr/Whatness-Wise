Physics

Physics is the fundamental branch of science that analyzes the behavior of matter, energy, space, and time through the application of universal laws and mathematical models.

Category
Academic discipline
Identity
The systematic study of the fundamental constituents of the universe and their interactions.
Mechanism
The application of mathematical models and empirical observation to quantify the behavior of matter, energy, space, and time.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular, as it uses 'nature' to define a field that studies the nature of things, and it fails to distinguish physics from chemistry or biology, which also study matter and energy.

Usually defined as: The branch of science concerned with the nature and properties of matter and energy.
