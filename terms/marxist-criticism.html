Marxist criticism

Literary Studies

Marxist criticism is an analytical framework that interprets cultural texts by examining how they reflect or challenge the economic power dynamics and class-based material conditions of their time.

Category
Critical Theory
Identity
An analytical framework for interpreting cultural artifacts.
Mechanism
Evaluating the relationship between a text's narrative content and the underlying economic power structures of the society that produced it.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to distinguish the method from general social commentary or other forms of political critique by omitting the specific focus on materialist dialectics.

Usually defined as: A type of literary criticism that interprets texts through the lens of class struggle and material conditions.
