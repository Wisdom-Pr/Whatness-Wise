Soil pollution

Environmental Science

Soil pollution is the accumulation of persistent synthetic or hazardous substances in the land that disrupt the natural chemical and biological processes necessary for healthy ecosystem function.

Category
Environmental degradation
Identity
The accumulation of hazardous substances in the pedosphere.
Mechanism
The introduction of synthetic chemicals, industrial waste, or excessive agricultural inputs that degrade the natural biological and chemical composition of the land.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between natural mineral toxicity and human-induced contamination, and it lacks the mechanism of persistence that differentiates pollution from temporary chemical exposure.

Usually defined as: The presence of toxic chemicals or other contaminants in the soil at high enough concentrations to pose a risk to human health or the ecosystem.
