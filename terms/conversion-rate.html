Conversion rate

Marketing

Conversion rate is the ratio of successful outcomes to the total number of opportunities, calculated by dividing the number of completed desired actions by the total number of visitors or participants in a given period.

Category
Performance metric
Identity
A ratio representing the frequency of a specific outcome relative to the total number of opportunities.
Mechanism
Dividing the number of successful conversions by the total number of visitors or potential leads.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to specify that the 'desired action' must be measured against a defined population sample, leading to ambiguity between conversion volume and conversion efficiency.

Usually defined as: The percentage of users who take a desired action.
