Welding

Mechanical Engineering

Welding is a fabrication process that joins materials by inducing coalescence through the application of heat, pressure, or a combination of both, with or without the use of a filler material.

Category
Manufacturing process
Identity
A fabrication technique for creating a permanent joint between materials.
Mechanism
Coalescence achieved through the application of heat, pressure, or both, often with a filler material.
Resolved
The standard definition incorrectly implies that melting is always required, ignoring solid-state welding processes like friction or ultrasonic welding.

Usually defined as: The process of joining two materials, usually metals, by heating them to their melting point.
