Labor market

Economics

A labor market is the economic system in which individuals offer their services for wages and employers compete to hire them, with the price and quantity of labor determined by the interaction of supply and demand.

Category
Economic system
Identity
A marketplace for the exchange of human services for compensation.
Mechanism
The interaction of supply and demand between individuals seeking employment and organizations seeking to hire.
Resolved
The standard definition often implies a physical location, failing to account for remote or decentralized digital platforms, and fails to distinguish between the 'market' as a mechanism and the 'labor' as the commodity.

Usually defined as: The place where workers and employers meet to exchange labor for wages.
