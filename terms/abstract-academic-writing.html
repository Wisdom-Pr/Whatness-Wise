Abstract (academic writing)

Research Methods
Also: abstract

An abstract is a concise, standalone summary of a complete research work that provides a condensed overview of the study's purpose, methods, findings, and implications.

Category
Documentary component
Identity
A concise, standalone summary of a complete research work.
Mechanism
Synthesizing the research question, methodology, results, and conclusions into a single paragraph.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish an abstract from an introduction or a conclusion, which also outline main points.

Usually defined as: A summary of a research paper or article that outlines the main points and findings.
