Equilibrium

Structural Engineering

Equilibrium is a state of a system where all opposing forces, influences, or variables are perfectly balanced, resulting in no net change to the system's overall condition.

Category
State of a system
Identity
A condition where all acting influences cancel each other out
Mechanism
The net sum of all forces, torques, or market variables equals zero
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify that the system must be in a state of rest or steady-state, and it confuses the condition of balance with the result of zero net change.

Usually defined as: A state in which opposing forces or influences are balanced.
