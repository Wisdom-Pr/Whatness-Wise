Inpatient care

Health Care
Also: inpatient

Inpatient care is the provision of medical treatment requiring formal admission to a healthcare facility for continuous clinical monitoring and access to specialized resources that cannot be safely managed in an outpatient setting.

Category
Healthcare service delivery
Identity
Medical care requiring formal admission to a facility
Mechanism
Continuous clinical monitoring and access to specialized equipment provided over an extended stay
Resolved
The standard definition relies on the 'overnight' condition, which fails to distinguish inpatient care from observation status or residential care; it also fails to specify the requirement of formal hospital admission.

Usually defined as: Medical treatment provided to a patient who is admitted to a hospital for at least one night.
