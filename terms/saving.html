Saving

Economics

Saving is the portion of disposable income not allocated to current consumption that is set aside to increase future purchasing power.

Category
Economic behavior
Identity
The portion of disposable income not spent on current consumption
Mechanism
deferring present expenditure to increase future purchasing power
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish saving from 'hoarding' or 'liquidity' and ignores the temporal aspect of the decision.

Usually defined as: the money one has left over after paying for expenses
