Cell division

Biology

Cell division is the biological process by which a parent cell replicates its genetic material and partitions its cytoplasm to produce two distinct daughter cells.

Category
Biological process
Identity
The replication and partitioning of genetic material and cytoplasm to produce two distinct units from a single parent entity.
Mechanism
The systematic segregation of replicated chromosomes followed by cytokinesis to ensure genetic continuity.
Resolved
The standard definition is circular, as it uses the word 'divides' to define 'cell division,' and it fails to distinguish between mitosis and meiosis by omitting the requirement of genetic preservation.

Usually defined as: The process by which a cell divides into two new daughter cells.
