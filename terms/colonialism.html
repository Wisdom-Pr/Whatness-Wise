Colonialism

History

Colonialism is a system of political and economic domination in which a foreign power exerts control over a territory and its people to extract resources and impose its own governance and cultural values.

Category
Political and economic system
Identity
A relationship of domination between an indigenous majority and a foreign minority.
Mechanism
The systematic imposition of external governance, cultural hegemony, and resource extraction to benefit the metropole.
Resolved
The standard definition often conflates the act of settlement with the broader structure of systemic exploitation, failing to distinguish it from mere migration or trade.

Usually defined as: The policy or practice of acquiring full or partial political control over another country, occupying it with settlers, and exploiting it economically.
