Confidentiality agreement

Law

A confidentiality agreement is a legally binding contract that prohibits the recipient from disclosing specified sensitive information to unauthorized parties in exchange for access to that information.

Category
Legal instrument
Identity
A binding contract that restricts the disclosure of specified information to unauthorized third parties.
Mechanism
Imposing a legal duty of non-disclosure on the recipient in exchange for access to proprietary or sensitive data.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish the agreement from a simple promise or a general duty of care by omitting the requirement of consideration and the specific scope of protected information.

Usually defined as: A contract between two parties to keep information secret.
