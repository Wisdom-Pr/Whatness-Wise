Glycemic index

Nutrition

Glycemic index is a relative numerical scale that ranks carbohydrate-containing foods based on how rapidly they raise blood glucose levels compared to a standard reference food like pure glucose.

Category
relative numerical scale
Identity
ranks carbohydrate-containing foods based on their immediate effect on blood glucose levels
Mechanism
comparing the rate and extent of blood sugar elevation caused by a food to that caused by an equal portion of a reference carbohydrate like pure glucose
Resolved
Standard definitions often fail to specify that the scale applies specifically to carbohydrates and measures blood glucose rise relative to a standardized reference food.

Usually defined as: A system that ranks foods on a scale based on how much they raise blood sugar levels.
