Chemical equilibrium

Chemistry

Chemical equilibrium is a dynamic state of a reversible reaction where the rate of the forward process equals the rate of the reverse process, resulting in constant macroscopic concentrations of all reactants and products.

Category
Dynamic state
Identity
A condition of a reversible reaction where the rate of the forward reaction equals the rate of the reverse reaction.
Mechanism
The balancing of opposing reaction rates resulting in constant macroscopic concentrations.
Resolved
The standard definition often fails to explicitly state that the reaction continues at the molecular level, potentially implying a static state rather than a dynamic one.

Usually defined as: Chemical equilibrium is the state in which both reactants and products are present in concentrations which have no further tendency to change with time.
