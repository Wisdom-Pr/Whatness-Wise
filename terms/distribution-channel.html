Distribution channel

Marketing

A distribution channel is a network of intermediaries that facilitates the transfer of ownership or access to a product or service from the producer to the final consumer.

Category
Business process
Identity
A network of interconnected intermediaries
Mechanism
facilitating the transfer of ownership and physical possession of goods from producers to end-users
Resolved
The standard definition fails to distinguish between the channel itself and the logistics of transportation, and it often ignores the role of service-based industries where no physical movement occurs.

Usually defined as: The path or route through which goods or services move from the producer to the final consumer.
