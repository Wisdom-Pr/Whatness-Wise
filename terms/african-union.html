African Union

International Relations
Also: AU, OAU

The African Union is an intergovernmental organization of sovereign states that coordinates collective policy and regional integration through a unified assembly to promote continental unity and security.

Category
Intergovernmental organization
Identity
A continental union of sovereign states
Mechanism
Coordinating collective policy through a unified assembly to promote regional integration and security
Resolved
The standard definition is purely descriptive and fails to distinguish the AU from other regional blocs like the Arab League or ECOWAS by omitting its specific mandate for continental integration and conflict resolution.

Usually defined as: An intergovernmental organization consisting of 55 member states located on the continent of Africa.
