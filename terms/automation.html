Automation

Computer Science

Automation is the application of self-regulating control systems that execute tasks through programmed logic and feedback loops to perform operations without continuous human intervention.

Category
Operational methodology
Identity
A system of self-regulating control mechanisms
Mechanism
executing pre-programmed logic or feedback loops to replace manual labor
Resolved
The standard definition is circular by implying 'automatic' operation and fails to distinguish automation from simple mechanization, which lacks the feedback-driven self-regulation inherent to true automation.

Usually defined as: The use of technology to perform tasks without human intervention.
