Motion

Physics

Motion is the continuous change in an object's position relative to a fixed reference frame over a measured interval of time.

Category
Physical state
Identity
A continuous change in the spatial coordinates of an object relative to a reference frame.
Mechanism
The displacement of an object across a duration of time, determined by the application of a net force.
Resolved
The standard definition fails to specify the necessity of a reference frame, making it relative and potentially circular if 'change' is interpreted as 'moving'.

Usually defined as: Motion is the change in position of an object over time.
