Core stability

Exercise Science

Core stability is the capacity of the trunk and hip musculature to maintain spinal alignment and resist unwanted movement under load, enabling efficient force transfer between the upper and lower body.

Category
Physical capacity
Identity
neuromuscular control of the lumbo-pelvic-hip complex
Mechanism
activating deep trunk muscles to maintain spinal alignment and resist unwanted movement under load
Resolved
Confuses dynamic neuromuscular control with simple muscle strength and fails to specify its primary biomechanical function of force transfer.

Usually defined as: The ability of the trunk muscles to stabilize the spine and pelvis during movement.
