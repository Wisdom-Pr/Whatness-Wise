Social status

Sociology

Social status is the relative position an individual or group occupies in a social hierarchy, defined by the level of honor, prestige, and respect accorded to them by others.

Category
relative social position
Identity
the level of social honor, prestige, or respect accorded to a role or individual
Mechanism
evaluated and recognized by others within a social hierarchy
Resolved
Standard definitions often rely on circular terms like 'standing' or fail to distinguish status (prestige and respect) from economic class or power.

Usually defined as: Social status is the social standing or rank of an individual or group within a society.
